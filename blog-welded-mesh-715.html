Introducing KESTREL WELDED MESH 715: Enhanced Performance for Demanding Applications

December 5, 2025 5 min read
Back to Blog & News

The Next Evolution in Welded Mesh Technology

The KESTREL WELDED MESH 715 series represents the latest addition to Kestrel Metal's comprehensive welded mesh portfolio, bringing a new level of versatility and engineering precision to the market. Building on the proven performance of the 711 through 714 series, the 715 introduces differentiated mesh patterns that combine the best attributes of its predecessors into a single, highly adaptable product. Engineered specifically for applications that demand both robust security and clear visibility, the 715 series addresses a long-standing challenge in the fencing and enclosure industry: how to provide effective containment at ground level without sacrificing the open sightlines that many agricultural, industrial, and residential applications require. The result is a product that sets a new benchmark for performance in the welded mesh category.

Key Features and Benefits

The 715 series incorporates several significant design advancements that distinguish it from standard welded mesh products. The differentiated mesh openings feature smaller apertures at the bottom of each panel, transitioning progressively to larger openings toward the top. This graduated design provides secure containment where it matters most at ground level, while the more open upper section maintains airflow, visibility, and aesthetic appeal. The enhanced PVC coating thickness of 0.6mm offers substantially improved corrosion resistance compared to the industry-standard 0.4mm coating, extending service life by an estimated 30 to 40 percent in exposed environments. Additionally, the 715 series is available in wider panel widths of up to 3000mm, reducing the number of posts and joints required for installation and lowering overall project costs. Improved weld joint strength, achieved through optimized electrical resistance welding parameters, ensures that each intersection point delivers consistent tensile performance even under heavy loads and dynamic stress conditions.

Applications and Use Cases

The KESTREL WELDED MESH 715 series has been designed to excel across a broad spectrum of demanding applications. In agricultural settings, the differentiated mesh pattern makes it ideal for livestock enclosures housing cattle, sheep, and horses, where smaller ground-level openings prevent hoof entrapment and predator intrusion while the larger upper mesh allows animals to see their surroundings, reducing stress and improving animal welfare. For crop protection in orchards and vineyards, the 715 provides an effective barrier against wildlife without obstructing sunlight penetration or airflow to the plants. In the industrial sector, the product serves as a reliable perimeter security solution for manufacturing facilities, warehouses, and logistics centers. Highway noise barriers benefit from the mesh's structural rigidity and ability to support acoustic panels, while residential boundary fencing applications take advantage of the clean, modern aesthetic and the balance between privacy and openness that the graduated mesh pattern provides.

The true strength of the 715 series lies in its versatility. A single product that can transition seamlessly from a high-security industrial perimeter to an aesthetically pleasing residential boundary fence represents a significant advancement in welded mesh design.

Technical Specifications

The KESTREL WELDED MESH 715 series is available in a comprehensive range of standard panel dimensions to suit common installation requirements. Wire diameters range from 2.5mm for lighter-duty applications to 5.0mm for heavy-duty security and industrial uses, with intermediate sizes available in 0.5mm increments. Mesh sizes are specified with differentiated openings: 50mm x 100mm at the lower section transitioning to 100mm x 200mm at the upper section, though custom mesh configurations can be produced to meet specific project requirements. Standard panel heights range from 1030mm to 2430mm, with widths available up to 3000mm. The product is offered in four standard PVC coating colors: green, black, gray, and brown, all manufactured using UV-stabilized compounds that resist fading and degradation under prolonged sun exposure. All coatings and materials comply with European environmental standards, including RoHS and REACH directives, ensuring that the 715 series meets the regulatory requirements of markets worldwide.
