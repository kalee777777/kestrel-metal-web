Product Information

The KESTREL WELDED MESH 712 (Combiplax series) features a differentiated mesh pattern with alternating mesh sizes within a single panel. The bottom section uses a smaller mesh (50 × 50 mm) for enhanced security at the base, while the top section features a larger mesh (75 × 75 mm) for improved visibility and airflow.

Each wire is first galvanized and then coated with a 0.4 - 0.6 mm PVC layer, providing double-layer protection against corrosion, UV radiation, and chemical exposure. This unique combination creates a highly versatile and durable fencing solution.
