Product Information

Electric resistance welding joins wire intersections by passing an electrical current through the crossing wires while applying controlled pressure. The heat generated at the contact point melts the wire surfaces, and the pressure forges them together into a solid weld joint.

Weld quality is critical to the overall performance of welded wire mesh — weak or inconsistent welds can lead to premature failure under load, reduce structural integrity, and compromise the long-term durability of the mesh in service.
