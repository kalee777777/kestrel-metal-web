Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about fence accessories, fasteners, and gabion connecting components.

Which post cap styles are available?
We offer flat, pyramid, ball, and gothic post caps in hot-dip galvanized, green, or black finish. They are sized to suit round, square, and rectangular posts from 40mm to 80mm. Caps protect post tops from moisture and debris while giving the fence a clean, finished appearance.
What clamp styles do you supply and for which posts?
We supply metal clamps, plastic clamps, U-bolt clamps, and brackets in sizes from 40mm to 80mm. They are designed to fix mesh, rail, and panels securely to round, square, or rectangular posts. Provide your post profile and size so we recommend and supply the correct clamp.
What bolt grades are available for fastening?
Hex bolts, nuts, and washers are available from Grade 4.8 high-carbon steel for general use, up to Grade 8.8 for high-load applications, and stainless steel for marine and corrosive environments. Sizes range from M6 to M12. Choose the grade based on tension load and exposure conditions.
How are gabion box panels connected?
Gabion panels are typically joined using lacing wire for secure edge seams, helical and spiral wire for quick even fastening, or C rings, U clips, and hook connections for specific joints and corners. The right method balances installation speed, connection strength, and coating protection.
What is lacing wire used for?
Lacing wire is a flexible, hot-dip galvanized wire used to bind gabion panels together along their edges, creating a secure, continuous connection that keeps stone fill contained. It is threaded through adjacent panel edges rather than wound fully around, saving material and time during assembly.
Are accessories available in colors to match my fence?
Yes. Caps, clamps, and brackets are available in hot-dip galvanized, green, and black PVC coated finishes to match common chain link and welded fence systems. Custom RAL colors can be produced on request for larger quantities, so tell us your fence coating when ordering.
Do your accessories come as complete fixing kits?
Yes. We can package pre-assembled fixing kits — bolts, nuts, washers, and clamps together — for each panel or post, simplifying on-site installation and reducing the risk of missing hardware. Tell us your fence type and panel count for a tailored bill of materials.
How long do galvanized accessories last?
Hot-dip galvanized accessories typically provide 25+ years of corrosion protection in most environments; stainless steel lasts even longer. The zinc coating offers sacrificial protection, so choosing the correct thickness and keeping coatings intact during installation maximizes service life.
