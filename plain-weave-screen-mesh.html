Plain Weave Construction

Plain weave is the most fundamental and widely used weave pattern in wire mesh manufacturing. In this weave, each warp wire (running vertically) passes alternately over and under each weft wire (running horizontally), creating a simple over-under-over-under pattern. This interlocking structure forms uniform square openings that are consistent across the entire mesh surface.

The balanced construction of plain weave provides excellent dimensional stability, making it ideal for applications where consistent aperture size is critical. The equal distribution of wire material in both warp and weft directions results in a mesh that maintains its shape under tension and offers reliable performance across a broad range of industries.

Applications

Plain weave screen mesh is the go-to choice for countless applications across industries. It is extensively used in food processing for sieving and filtering, in industrial settings for particle separation and screening, in architectural projects for decorative facades and sunscreens, and in window and door manufacturing for insect screening. Its versatility, combined with the availability of different mesh counts and wire diameters, makes plain weave the most widely adopted weave pattern for stainless steel screen mesh worldwide.
