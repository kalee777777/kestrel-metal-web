Product Information

Gabion retaining structures are engineered wire mesh systems designed to provide effective soil stabilization and slope protection for a wide range of civil engineering and landscape applications. Constructed from high-quality welded steel wire, these structures are filled with locally sourced natural stone to create robust, long-lasting retaining walls.

The modular nature of gabion retaining walls allows them to accommodate uneven terrain and ground movement without compromising structural integrity. Their permeable design ensures excellent drainage, preventing the buildup of hydrostatic pressure behind the wall that can lead to failure in conventional concrete structures.

Applications

Gabion retaining structures are extensively used in highway embankments, bridge abutments, residential terracing, commercial developments, and riverbank stabilization projects. Their versatility and adaptability make them suitable for both small-scale landscaping projects and large-scale infrastructure applications.
