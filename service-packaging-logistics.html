What We Offer

Packaging & Shipping Solutions

01

Protective Packaging Systems

Every product is packaged according to its shape, weight, and surface finish. Wire mesh panels are separated with protective interleaving paper and bundled with steel strapping. Powder-coated and galvanized items receive corner protectors and foam wrapping. Posts and rails are bundled in manageable weights with labeled tags for easy identification on site.

02

Custom Crating & Palletizing

For high-value, oversized, or internationally shipped orders, we build custom wooden crates and pallets engineered to withstand rough handling and multi-modal transit. Each crate is stamped with handling instructions, weight, and dimensional data for efficient freight processing and customs clearance.

03

Domestic & International Freight

We arrange Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) and Full Truckload (FTL) shipments across North America, as well as ocean freight and air freight for international orders. Our logistics team selects the most cost-effective carrier and route for each shipment, balancing speed, cost, and handling care.

04

Scheduled Site Delivery

For large construction projects, we coordinate delivery schedules to match your installation sequence and site storage capacity. Products can be staged and shipped in phased deliveries so that materials arrive when needed, reducing on-site storage requirements and the risk of damage from prolonged exposure.

Our Process

How We Manage Your Shipment

1

Order & Packaging Plan

Once your order is ready, our packaging team reviews the product list, finish types, and destination to determine the optimal packaging method, crate design, and shipping configuration.

2

Packaging & Labeling

Products are carefully packed, separated, and secured using protective materials. Each package is labeled with part numbers, quantities, and handling instructions for easy receiving and inventory management.

3

Carrier Coordination

We select the best carrier based on transit time, cost, and destination requirements. Freight is booked, Bills of Lading are prepared, and pickup is scheduled to align with your delivery window.

4

Tracking & Confirmation

Shipments are tracked from departure through delivery. You receive proactive updates with tracking numbers and estimated arrival times. After delivery, we confirm receipt and address any concerns promptly.
