Product Information

PVC coated chain link fencing combines the strength of galvanized steel with a protective polymer coating for enhanced aesthetics and durability. Available in residential polyester coated and commercial 2nd generation fusion bonded for maximum coating adhesion and longevity.

Benefits

  • ✓Multiple colors for aesthetic integration with surroundings
  • ✓Dual-layer protection — galvanized core plus polymer outer coating
  • ✓Superior corrosion and UV resistance
  • ✓Fusion bonded or polyester coated options
  • ✓Low maintenance — easy to clean with water hose
  • ✓Resistant to peeling, cracking, and fading

Applications

Residential communities, school campuses, park boundaries, commercial properties, swimming pool enclosures, tennis courts.
