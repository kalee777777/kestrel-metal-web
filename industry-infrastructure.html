FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What height is required for airport perimeter fencing?
FAA Advisory Circular 107-1 and ICAO Annex 14 typically require perimeter fencing of at least 2.4 meters (8 feet) height with anti-climb mesh design. Some jurisdictions require 3-meter fencing with topping. We manufacture to your specific airport authority requirements.
Can your fencing withstand vehicle impact?
Yes. We supply crash-rated fencing systems and median barriers designed to meet AASHTO MASH impact standards. These systems are engineered to stop or redirect vehicles at designated threat levels for highway and critical infrastructure protection.
Do you provide anti-throw screens for bridges?
Yes. Our bridge anti-throw screens are manufactured from galvanized or PVC-coated mesh with apertures small enough to prevent objects from being dropped onto traffic below. Custom mounting systems are available for various bridge railing designs.
How do you handle large-scale infrastructure projects?
We have extensive experience supplying fencing for kilometers-long infrastructure perimeters. Our production capacity of 50,000+ tons annually, combined with dedicated project logistics, ensures consistent supply for large-scale government and transportation contracts.
