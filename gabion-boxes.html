Product Information

Welded gabion boxes are rectangular wire mesh containers manufactured from high-quality steel wire, welded at each intersection to form a rigid, durable structure. These versatile units are filled with natural stone or rock to create heavy, stable modules suitable for a wide range of civil engineering and environmental applications.

Available in galvanized or PVC coated finishes, welded gabion boxes provide excellent corrosion resistance for long-term performance in harsh environments. Their welded construction offers superior strength and dimensional accuracy compared to traditional woven gabions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between welded and woven gabion boxes? +
Welded gabion boxes are made from straight steel wires welded at intersections, creating a rigid, rectangular structure ideal for retaining walls and architectural features. Woven gabion boxes use double-twisted hexagonal mesh that is more flexible and better suited for hydraulic applications like riverbank protection. Welded boxes offer a cleaner appearance, while woven boxes handle ground settlement better.
What stone size should I use to fill gabion boxes? +
The ideal stone size should be 1.5 to 2 times the mesh opening size to prevent stone from falling through. For our standard 50mm mesh, use stones between 80-150mm. For 75mm mesh, use 100-200mm stones. Avoid using elongated or flat stones as they create large voids. Crushed granite, basalt, or limestone are commonly used fill materials.

How do I install welded gabion boxes for a retaining wall? +
Installation follows these steps: (1) Prepare a level foundation of compacted gravel or concrete. (2) Assemble the gabion box by unfolding and connecting panels with helical wires or C-rings. (3) Place the box on the foundation and secure to adjacent units. (4) Fill with stone in layers of 250-300mm, hand-placing the front face for a neat appearance. (5) Close the lid and secure with fasteners. (6) Repeat for multi-tier walls, staggering joints like brickwork.
Can gabion boxes be used in coastal and marine environments? +
Yes. For coastal and marine applications, we recommend our PVC-coated gabion boxes which provide a double layer of protection — galvanized wire plus a durable polymer coating. This combination offers 50+ years of service life even in saltwater environments. We also offer heavy-duty Type C gabion boxes with thicker wire for high-energy coastal zones.
