Product Information

PVC coated hexagonal wire netting is manufactured by applying a layer of PVC (polyvinyl chloride) coating over galvanized steel wire, then twisting it into the characteristic hexagonal pattern. The PVC coating provides an additional protective barrier and is available in multiple colors such as green, black, blue, and yellow.

The dual-layer protection system — galvanized core wire plus PVC outer coating — ensures superior durability and weather resistance. The smooth PVC surface also enhances the aesthetic appearance, making it an ideal choice for applications where visual appeal is as important as functionality.
