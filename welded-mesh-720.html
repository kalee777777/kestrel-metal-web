Product Information

KESTREL WELDED MESH 720 series is a high-security fencing system featuring integrated barbed wire welded directly into the mesh panel structure. This design creates a formidable physical barrier that deters unauthorized access and intrusion attempts.

Each intersection is precision-welded to ensure maximum structural integrity, while the galvanized coating provides long-lasting corrosion resistance even in harsh outdoor environments.
