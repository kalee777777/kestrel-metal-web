Product Overview

Accurate Filtration & Exceptional Durability

Stainless steel screen mesh is a precision-woven wire mesh product manufactured from high-quality 304 and 316 grade stainless steel wire. It features uniform square openings that provide accurate particle separation, excellent corrosion resistance, and superior durability for demanding filtration, screening, and industrial applications across a wide range of industries.

From fine sub-micron filter elements to robust aggregate screens, the consistent aperture tolerance and hygienic, non-toxic surface make stainless screen mesh the material of choice for food, pharmaceutical, chemical, mining and architectural projects.

  • Uniform apertures from 1–500 mesh
  • AISI 304 & 316 stainless steel grades

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about stainless steel screen mesh.

What is the difference between 304 and 316 stainless steel?
Type 304 stainless steel contains 18% chromium and 8% nickel, offering excellent corrosion resistance for most general applications at lower cost. Type 316 stainless steel contains 16% chromium, 10% nickel, and 2% molybdenum, providing superior resistance to pitting corrosion in chloride-rich environments such as marine and coastal applications. For food, pharmaceutical and chemical processing where chloride or acid exposure is expected, 316 is recommended; for general indoor screening and architectural use, 304 performs excellently at a lower cost.
What mesh count do I need for my application?
The appropriate mesh count depends on the size of particles to separate or retain. For coarse screening, 10x10 to 20x20 mesh with apertures of 2.11mm to 0.93mm is suitable. For medium-fine filtration, 30x30 to 40x40 mesh provides apertures of 0.62mm to 0.41mm. For fine filtration such as pharmaceutical processing, 60x60 or higher with apertures of 0.27mm or smaller may be required. Consider flow rate, particle size distribution and required filtration efficiency when selecting your mesh count.
Can stainless steel screen mesh be welded or fabricated?
Yes, stainless steel screen mesh can be welded, cut, bent, and fabricated into various shapes and configurations. TIG welding is the preferred method, producing clean, precise welds without contaminating the material. Common options include cutting to custom sizes, welding into cylindrical filter elements, forming into baskets or cones, framing with perimeter edges, and assembling into multi-panel screens. Proper techniques maintain the integrity of the mesh openings and prevent wire distortion.
What temperature can stainless steel screen mesh withstand?
Type 304 stainless steel can withstand continuous service up to approximately 870°C (1600°F), while Type 316 has a slightly lower maximum of about 825°C (1520°F). For intermittent or short-term exposure, both grades handle higher temperatures. Prolonged high-temperature exposure may cause oxidation, scaling, or reduced mechanical strength, so consider alloy composition for applications above 600°C.
Is stainless steel screen mesh suitable for food contact applications?
Yes, both 304 and 316 grade stainless steel meet FDA, EFSA and other international food contact material standards. The non-porous, smooth surface prevents bacterial growth and is easy to clean and sanitize. It is used for food processing screens, brewing filters, juice and wine filtration, flour sifting and bakery production. For highly acidic foods or salt exposure, Type 316 is recommended. Material test certificates confirming standards compliance are supplied on request.
