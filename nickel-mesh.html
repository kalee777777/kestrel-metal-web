Nickel Alloy Properties

Nickel alloy wire mesh is woven from high-purity nickel and nickel-based superalloys including Monel 400, Hastelloy C-276, and Inconel 600. These materials offer outstanding resistance to corrosion, high temperatures, and chemical attack, making nickel mesh the preferred choice for the most demanding industrial environments where stainless steel grades fall short.

Each alloy is carefully selected to balance mechanical strength, ductility, and compatibility with specific process media — from caustic chemical streams to cryogenic hydrogen systems and high-temperature aerospace components.

Benefits

  • ✓Superior corrosion resistance in acidic, alkaline, and chloride environments.
  • ✓Stable mechanical properties at extreme temperatures from −200°C to 1200°C.
  • ✓Compatible with hydrogen and fuel cell applications without embrittlement.
  • ✓High tensile strength and dimensional stability under pressure.
  • ✓Excellent electrical conductivity for electrochemical and battery applications.
  • ✓Fully recyclable alloy supporting sustainable manufacturing practices.

Applications

Nickel alloy mesh is widely used in hydrogen energy systems including fuel cell electrodes and electrolyzer separators, chemical processing equipment such as filters and catalyst supports, aerospace component manufacturing requiring high-temperature and corrosion-resistant materials, and battery manufacturing for lithium-ion battery collector screens and solid-state battery separators.
