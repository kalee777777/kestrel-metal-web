Product Information

S-Knot fence features a smooth, curved knot design that eliminates sharp edges and prevents injury to animals. The distinctive S-shaped knot provides a secure attachment point between horizontal line wires and vertical stay wires while maintaining a smooth surface that is safe for livestock.

Ideal for farms where animal safety is a priority, the S-knot design reduces the risk of cuts and abrasions on animals that come into contact with the fence. The smooth knot also allows for easy installation and maintenance.

  • ✓Versatile for cattle, sheep, goats, and horses

Applications

Perfect for livestock farms, dairy operations, and animal sanctuaries where animal safety is paramount. The S-knot design is especially suitable for horses and other animals that may rub against or lean on the fence.
