Product Overview

Rigid, Precise, Built to Perform

Welded wire mesh panels are manufactured using precision automated welding machines that fuse each wire intersection, creating a rigid and dimensionally stable panel. Unlike woven mesh, the welded construction prevents movement of individual wires, ensuring consistent opening sizes and superior structural integrity.

These panels are widely used across fencing, construction reinforcement, industrial screening, and architectural applications where strength and accuracy are paramount. Available in galvanized, PVC coated, and stainless steel finishes, welded wire mesh panels deliver exceptional performance across diverse environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about welded wire mesh panels.

What are the standard sizes for welded wire mesh panels?
Standard welded wire mesh panels come in a variety of sizes, with the most common being 1m x 2m, 1.2m x 2.4m, 1.5m x 2.5m, 1.5m x 3m, 2m x 3m, and 2m x 4m. Custom panel sizes can also be manufactured to meet specific project requirements. Mesh openings range from 50mm x 50mm to 150mm x 300mm depending on the application.
What is the difference between welded and woven wire mesh?
Welded wire mesh is made by welding wires at each intersection point, creating a rigid, flat panel that maintains its shape under stress. Woven wire mesh is created by interlacing wires in an over-and-under pattern, resulting in a flexible material that can be rolled. Welded mesh offers greater structural strength and dimensional precision, while woven mesh provides more flexibility and is typically sold in rolls.
Which surface treatment is best for welded wire mesh panels?
The best surface treatment depends on the application and environment. Galvanized panels provide excellent rust resistance at a competitive price, ideal for general outdoor use. PVC coated panels offer superior corrosion protection and are available in various colors, making them popular for residential and architectural applications. Stainless steel panels provide the highest level of corrosion resistance and are recommended for coastal, chemical, or highly corrosive environments.
How do I install welded wire mesh panels?
Welded wire mesh panel installation is straightforward: 1) Set posts at appropriate intervals, 2) Secure posts in concrete footings, 3) Attach panels to posts using clips, brackets, or tie wire, 4) Ensure panels are level and properly aligned, 5) Install post caps and any additional hardware. Professional installation is recommended for large-scale or security-critical projects.
Can welded wire mesh panels be used for concrete reinforcement?
Yes, welded wire mesh panels are widely used for concrete reinforcement in construction. They provide uniform stress distribution across concrete slabs, foundations, walls, and pavements. The welded intersections ensure the mesh maintains its shape during concrete pouring and curing. Common specifications include panels with 100mm x 100mm or 150mm x 150mm openings using 4mm-8mm wire diameter.
What wire diameter should I choose for my application?
Wire diameter selection depends on the intended use and required strength. For light-duty applications such as garden fencing or animal enclosures, 3.0-4.0mm wire diameter is typically sufficient. For medium-duty applications like security fencing and general construction, 4.0-6.0mm wire provides good strength. For heavy-duty applications including infrastructure protection and highway barriers, 6.0-8.0mm wire diameter is recommended.
