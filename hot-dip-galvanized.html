Hot-Dip Galvanized

A durable zinc coating applied by immersion in molten zinc at 450°C, offering excellent sacrificial corrosion protection ideal for exposed, coastal, and industrial environments.

Hot-Dip Galvanized

Hot-dip galvanizing creates a metallurgically bonded zinc layer that sacrificially protects the underlying steel — the zinc corrodes preferentially even when the coating is scratched or abraded. Manufactured to ASTM A123 / EN ISO 1461 standards with a minimum zinc coating of 240g/m² (Class 3), this finish delivers 25+ years of maintenance-free service in moderate environments and 15-20 years in severe coastal zones.

Process

Galvanizing Process

Four-stage hot-dip galvanizing process ensuring uniform zinc coverage and metallurgical bonding.

1

Surface Preparation

Degreasing, acid pickling, and fluxing remove oxides and prepare the steel surface for optimal zinc adhesion.

2

Molten Zinc Immersion

Steel is immersed in molten zinc at 445-460°C, forming a series of zinc-iron alloy layers.

3

Controlled Withdrawal

Slow withdrawal allows excess zinc to drain off, ensuring uniform coating thickness and smooth finish.

4

Cooling & Inspection

Air cooling followed by coating thickness measurement and adhesion testing per ASTM A123.

Benefits

Key Advantages

Why choose hot-dip galvanizing for your fence posts.

Sacrificial Protection

Zinc corrodes preferentially to steel, protecting exposed areas even if coating is damaged.

25+ Year Service Life

Proven field performance delivering decades of maintenance-free protection in moderate environments.

Self-Healing

Zinc oxidation products fill minor scratches and abrasions, preventing rust initiation.

Uniform Coverage

Complete immersion ensures full coverage including interior surfaces and hard-to-reach areas.

Cost-Effective

Lower initial cost compared to PVC or powder coating with excellent lifecycle value.

Environmentally Safe

Zinc is a naturally occurring element, fully recyclable with no toxic byproducts.
