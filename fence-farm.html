Farm Fence Features

Engineered for the demands of agriculture with reinforced knots, graduated spacing, and heavy galvanized protection.

Safety, Security & Durability

Braided wire withstands pressure from large herds. Heavy-duty knots and strong wire keep cattle worry-free, while closely spaced bottom wires protect young animals from being trapped or escaping.

Versatile Application

Ideally suited for corralling cattle and bison, and equally effective for pigs, horses, sheep, goats, and dogs. Ideal for large ranches and capable of handling crowded cattle pens.

Built-in Benefits

Quality steel selection guarantees reliable performance. Elastic design resists failure and prevents animal injury, while special crimps adapt to changing weather conditions.

Three knot options handle animal pressure, predation, terrain, and weather. Special crimps expand and contract with the seasons to maintain tension and integrity over time.

Multiple spacing options, most featuring graduated spacing to deter small animals. Pull-out spacing from 6" to 16" with heights ranging from 26" to 49".

Galvanized Wire

Standard 12–1/2 gauge wire with low or high tensile strength options for light and heavy applications. All wire is galvanized with a protective zinc layer to resist rust and corrosion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about farm fence selection, installation, and livestock containment.

What height farm fence do I need for my livestock?
Farm fence heights range from 39" to 96" (100–244cm). Cattle and general livestock typically need 48"–54"; sheep and goats are well-served by 39"–48" with tight bottom spacing; horses do best with 54"–60" non-climb mesh; deer fencing requires extra-tall panels of 72" and above. Choose based on your specific animals and escape risk.
What is the difference between hinge joint and fixed knot?
Hinge joint knots allow the fence to flex and spring back under animal pressure, ideal for cattle and general livestock where some give is desirable. Fixed knot provides maximum rigidity and stays put even under heavy tension, better for demanding containment needs where the fence must hold its shape. S-knot offers a smooth finish that minimizes animal injury.
How long does a galvanized farm fence last?
Farm fence with heavy galvanized coating (Class 3) can last 25 to 50 years depending on environment and maintenance. The zinc layer provides sacrificial corrosion protection; in normal rural conditions expect several decades of service. Regular inspection, proper tensioning, and timely repairs of broken wires will maximize the lifespan of your fence.
What roll lengths are available?
Farm fence rolls are available in 100m, 200m, and 330m lengths. The ideal roll length depends on your fence line and terrain. Longer rolls reduce joints and installation time for straight runs, while shorter rolls are easier to handle on uneven or hilly ground. Contact us to plan the most efficient roll lengths for your acreage.
What is graduated spacing and why does it matter?
Graduated spacing places horizontal wires closer together at the bottom of the fence and wider apart toward the top. This design prevents small animals like lambs, kids, and rabbits from squeezing through lower gaps while reducing material cost. Pull-out spacing typically ranges from 6" at the bottom to 16" near the top.
What wire gauge and tensile strength should I choose?
Standard farm fence uses 12–1/2 gauge line wire with a minimum tensile strength of 550 MPa. High-tensile steel provides greater strength and allows longer spans with fewer posts, ideal for large acreage. Low-carbon options offer more ductility and ease of tying, suited to light applications. All wire is galvanized to resist rust.
Can farm fence be used for horses?
Yes. The Non-Climb Horse Fence uses a 2"×4" mesh pattern that prevents hooves from getting caught while maintaining clear sight lines for horses. Alternatively, hinge joint or fixed knot fence with closely spaced wires works well for horse paddocks. Always ensure smooth, injury-free knots and proper tension to keep horses safe.
What steel is used for farm fence wire?
KESTREL METAL manufactures farm fence from high-quality low-carbon and high-tensile steel wire. All wire is galvanized with a heavy zinc coating (Class 3) for maximum corrosion resistance. In-plant alloy control eliminates impurities and guarantees consistent tensile strength, preventing sagging, breakage, and premature fence failure.
