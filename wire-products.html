WIRE PRODUCTS

High-security wire products engineered for perimeter security, agricultural boundaries and critical infrastructure protection. Our product range includes traditional twisted barbed wire for cost-effective agricultural fencing, and high-security razor wire with concertina coils for military, prison and high-security perimeter systems. All products are manufactured using high-tensile steel wire with hot-dip galvanized or zinc-aluminum coated finishes for maximum strength, corrosion resistance and long service life in demanding environments.
