PVC Coated

A smooth polymer layer extruded over a galvanized steel core provides dual-layer protection against corrosion, UV degradation, and impact with color options including green, black, and custom shades.

PVC Coated

PVC coating begins with a fully galvanized steel core that provides primary structural corrosion resistance, which is then extrusion-coated with a UV-stabilized polymer layer acting as a physical barrier against salt spray, moisture, and UV radiation. Premium PVC formulations with UV inhibitors and impact modifiers deliver 30+ year service life in coastal environments, making this the preferred choice where maximum longevity and aesthetics are required.

Process

PVC Coating Process

Multi-stage extrusion process applying UV-stable polymer over galvanized steel core.

1

Galvanized Core Prep

Hot-dip galvanized steel core is cleaned and preheated to ensure optimal polymer adhesion.

2

Polymer Extrusion

UV-stabilized PVC compound is extruded onto the rotating steel core under controlled temperature.

3

Cooling & Sizing

Water quenching solidifies the coating, followed by diameter sizing to ensure dimensional accuracy.

4

Quality Testing

Adhesion testing, thickness measurement, and UV stability verification per EN 10245-2.
