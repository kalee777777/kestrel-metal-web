Welded Razor Mesh

Razor wire welded into rigid mesh panels for permanent fencing. Welded razor mesh combines the security of razor wire with the structural rigidity of welded mesh panels. The razor wire strips are welded to a steel frame at uniform intervals, creating a permanent, high-security barrier that is virtually impossible to cut or climb without specialized tools.

Product Information

Welded razor mesh is a type of security fencing made by welding razor wire onto steel mesh panels. The razor blades are welded at each intersection point, creating a rigid and impenetrable barrier that provides excellent security protection.

Compared with traditional chain link fences or barbed wire fences, welded razor mesh offers superior anti-climb and anti-cut performance, making it an ideal choice for high-security applications.

Benefits

  • ✓Rigid structure provides permanent and reliable security protection.
  • ✓Sharp razor blades welded at intersection points offer excellent anti-climb and anti-cut performance.
  • ✓Hot dip galvanized steel or stainless steel materials ensure great corrosion resistance and long service life.
  • ✓Available in both panel and roll forms for flexible installation options.
  • ✓Various aperture sizes and blade types can be customized to meet different security requirements.

Application

Welded razor mesh is widely used in military bases, prisons, airports, government buildings, industrial facilities, warehouses, residential communities and other places requiring high-level security protection.
