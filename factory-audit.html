FACTORY AUDIT & PLANT VISIT

Schedule an on-site audit or guided tour of Kestrel Metal’s manufacturing facility in Anping, Hebei. International buyers, third-party auditors, and inspection agencies are welcome.

Kestrel Metal’s ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing facility in Anping, Hebei, China spans 15,000+ square meters and operates production lines for chain link fence, 3D welded wire panels, 358 security mesh, gabion boxes, razor wire (NATO-22/BTO-22), barbed wire, epoxy coated wire mesh, and stainless steel wire mesh. The factory employs 60+ staff, maintains 40+ patents, and ships to 50+ countries with an annual capacity of 36,000+ metric tons. Overseas buyers, auditors, and inspection agencies are invited to schedule factory audits and plant visits.
