A baked, UV-stable polymer finish electrostatically applied to provide long-lasting color retention and superior scratch resistance for a premium, professional appearance.

Powder Coated

Powder coating applies dry polymer powder electrostatically to the steel surface, then cures it at 180-200°C to form a continuous, uniform film. The result is a hard, durable finish with excellent color retention, UV stability, and scratch resistance. Available in standard RAL colors including black (RAL 9005), gray (RAL 7016), green (RAL 6005), and custom shades, this finish is ideal for architectural, commercial, and residential applications where aesthetics are paramount.

Process

Powder Coating Process

Four-stage electrostatic application and thermal curing process for uniform, durable finish.

1

Surface Preparation

Phosphate conversion coating and chromate pretreatment ensure maximum powder adhesion and corrosion resistance.

2

Electrostatic Application

Dry polymer powder is sprayed with electrostatic charge onto the grounded steel surface.

3

Thermal Curing

Coated parts enter a curing oven at 180-200°C where powder melts, flows, and cross-links into a continuous film.

4

Cooling & Inspection

Air cooling followed by thickness measurement, adhesion testing, and visual color verification.
