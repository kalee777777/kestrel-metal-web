Product Information

Single twist barbed wire is the most basic and commonly used type of barbed wire. It consists of a single strand of wire with barbs wrapped around it at regular intervals. The barbs are formed by twisting a short piece of wire around the main strand and then cutting it to create sharp points.

This type of barbed wire is lightweight, easy to install and cost-effective, making it an ideal choice for agricultural fencing, temporary enclosures and general-purpose perimeter protection.

Benefits

  • ✓Most economical barbed wire type for large area fencing.
  • ✓Lightweight and easy to handle, transport and install.
  • ✓Available in various wire gauges to suit different applications.
  • ✓Galvanized coating provides excellent corrosion resistance.
  • ✓Barb spacing can be customized from 50mm to 150mm.

Application

Single twist barbed wire is widely used in agriculture farming, livestock fencing, grassland boundary, private property, highway and railway fencing, and other general-purpose fencing applications.
