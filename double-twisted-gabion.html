Product Information

Double twisted gabion baskets are manufactured from hexagonal double-twisted woven wire mesh, providing a versatile and durable solution for a wide range of civil engineering and environmental applications. The hexagonal mesh pattern is formed by twisting pairs of wires together at least three times, creating a robust interlocking structure that resists deformation and prevents unravelling even if individual wires are cut or broken.

The inherent flexibility of the double-twist weaving process allows gabion baskets to deform under load without losing structural integrity, making them particularly effective in applications where ground movement or settlement is expected. This flexibility also enables gabion structures to conform to uneven terrain, providing superior performance compared to rigid concrete alternatives.

Application

Double twisted gabion baskets are extensively used in retaining walls, erosion control, river training works, landscaping, coastal protection, and military fortifications. Their versatility and adaptability make them suitable for both small-scale residential projects and large-scale infrastructure applications.
