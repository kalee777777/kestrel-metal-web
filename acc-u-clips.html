Product Information

U Clips are U-shaped fastening clips designed specifically for reinforcing gabion panel intersections and securing internal diaphragms. Their robust U-shape provides excellent grip on wire mesh intersections, preventing panel separation under load and ensuring the gabion structure maintains its intended shape.

Made from galvanized low carbon steel with a hot-dip galvanized or PVC coated finish, U Clips offer durable corrosion resistance. They are available in various wire diameters and leg lengths to suit different gabion panel thicknesses and project requirements.

Assembly Instructions

Step-by-step guide for gabion box assembly using U clips

1Prepare Materials

Prepare all needed gabions, U clips, lacing wires and tools. Unfold and remove each gabion onto a flat hard surface.

2Erect Each Section

Form an open box by erecting the sides, ends and diaphragms vertically.

3Reinforce Panels with U Clips

Secure panel intersections and diaphragm connections using U clips. Attach clips at each crossing point for maximum reinforcement.

4Install Stiffeners

At 1/3 and 2/3 depth of fill, place stiffeners from front to back to prevent local deformation.

5Fill the Stones

Fill gabions with hard, durable stones. Place stones manually to minimize voids and ensure a neat appearance.

6Cover Lid with U Clips

Place the top lid panel and secure with U clips and lacing wire around all edges, ensuring a tight and uniform closure.
