Frequently Asked Questions

What wire gauge is used for galvanized chain link fence? +
Kestrel Metal manufactures galvanized chain link fence in wire gauges from 6 to 12.5 BWG (Basic Wire Gauge). For residential applications, 9-10.5 gauge is most common, while commercial and security applications typically use 6-8 gauge wire for added strength. Higher gauge numbers indicate thinner wire. We recommend consulting our specifications table to select the appropriate gauge for your project requirements.
What is the difference between hot-dip and electro galvanized? +
Hot-dip galvanizing immerses the wire in molten zinc at approximately 450°C, creating a thick, durable coating (40-270 g/m²) that provides superior corrosion resistance lasting 20+ years. Electro galvanizing uses an electric current to apply a thinner zinc layer, offering a smoother finish at lower cost but with less corrosion protection. For coastal or high-humidity environments, we strongly recommend hot-dip galvanized chain link fence.
Does galvanized chain link fence meet ASTM A392 standards? +
Yes. All Kestrel Metal galvanized chain link fences comply with ASTM A392 standard for zinc-coated steel chain link fence fabric. We also meet BS EN 10223-7 European standards. Each batch is tested for zinc coating adhesion, tensile strength, and mesh dimensional accuracy. We can provide test certificates and material reports with every order.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
Our standard MOQ for galvanized chain link fence is 500 square meters for bulk orders. However, we support trial orders for new customers with a minimum of 200 square meters. Contact us for custom MOQ arrangements based on your specific project requirements and destination.
Can I customize the mesh opening size and fence height? +
Absolutely. We offer mesh openings from 3/8 inch to 2 inches (12.7mm to 50.8mm) and standard heights from 3ft to 12ft (0.9m to 3.6m). Custom sizes outside these ranges are available upon request. Our production lines support flexible configurations to meet your exact project specifications.
How long does galvanized chain link fence last? +
A hot-dip galvanized chain link fence typically lasts 20-30 years depending on environmental conditions. In inland areas with normal humidity, 25+ years is common. In coastal areas with salt exposure, the lifespan may be reduced to 15-20 years. For maximum longevity in harsh environments, consider our PVC-coated chain link fence which adds a protective polymer layer over the galvanized wire.
