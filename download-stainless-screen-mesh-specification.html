Detailed specification of stainless steel screen mesh including grades 304/316, aperture sizes, wire diameters, and mesh counts.

Product Overview

This document provides complete technical specifications for Kestrel Metal's range of wire mesh products and fencing solutions.

Material Specifications

PropertyValue
MaterialLow carbon steel wire, stainless steel 304/316, galvanized steel
Wire diameter range0.5mm - 12.7mm (SWG 4 - 32)
Tensile strength350 - 700 N/mm² (depending on material and diameter)
Elongation8% - 25% (ASTM A370)
StandardsASTM A185, ASTM A1064, BS 4483, ISO 9001:2015

Available Variants

  • Welded Wire Mesh — Aperture range: 6.4mm × 6.4mm to 150mm × 150mm. Panel sizes: 2.0m × 3.0m, 2.4m × 3.6m, custom sizes available. Wire diameter: 1.2mm to 8.0mm.
  • Woven Wire Mesh — Mesh count: 2 × 2 to 100 × 100 (per inch). Wire diameter: 0.1mm to 3.0mm. Weave types: Plain weave, twill weave, Dutch weave.
  • Expanded Metal Mesh — Strand width: 1.0mm to 6.0mm. Opening size: 6mm × 12mm to 50mm × 100mm. Sheet sizes: 1.2m × 2.4m, 1.5m × 3.0m.
