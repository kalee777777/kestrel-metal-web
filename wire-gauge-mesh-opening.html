Product Information

Wire gauge directly determines the mechanical strength and application suitability of welded wire mesh. The wire diameter dictates the overall rigidity of the mesh structure — thicker wires provide higher tensile strength and resistance to bending or impact loads.

Mesh opening is measured center-to-center between adjacent wires, not the clear gap. As a general rule, the mesh opening should not exceed 4–5 times the wire diameter to ensure adequate structural integrity and load distribution across the mesh panel.

Applications

Wire gauge and mesh opening selection is critical for poultry cages, garden and agricultural fencing, construction reinforcement, and high-security perimeter fencing — each requiring a different balance of visibility, strength, and cost.
