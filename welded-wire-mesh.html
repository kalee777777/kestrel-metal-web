Product Formats and Standard Specifications

Welded wire mesh is supplied in three formats, each engineered for a distinct set of applications. Rigid welded panels (including the 700 series — 711, 712, 713, 715 and 717 configurations) are pre-fabricated to fixed dimensions with precision resistance-welded joints, used where flatness and load distribution matter: fencing systems, reinforcement, and architectural screening. Welded mesh rolls are the flexible, cost-efficient format for large-area installation. Welded gabion boxes and mattresses convert welded mesh into erosion-control and retaining structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Rolls or panels — which should I order?

Rolls suit large-area coverage where the mesh can be cut to length on site and attached to line posts; panels suit applications demanding a rigid, flat surface with clean edges — fencing systems, architectural screens, and reinforced enclosures. Panels also install faster because no tensioning is required.

What coating weight do I need?

For inland, dry environments, standard commercial coatings (around 60–120 g/m²) deliver 15–25 years of service. Coastal, high-humidity or chemically exposed sites should specify heavier coatings toward 240–270 g/m² or a PVC layer over galvanizing. Always require the minimum zinc weight in g/m² in writing rather than the word “galvanized”.

Can custom panel sizes be produced?

Yes. Custom widths, lengths, mesh configurations and non-standard apertures are manufactured to order; discuss lead time at quotation stage because custom tooling extends the standard 15–25 day window.
