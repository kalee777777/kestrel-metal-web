Product Information

Y-post security fence system uses Y-shaped steel posts with anti-climb mesh panels, providing maximum perimeter security for airports, military installations, prisons, and critical infrastructure.

The unique Y-shaped post design creates an effective physical barrier that prevents climbing and unauthorized access. Combined with high-tensile anti-climb mesh, this system delivers robust perimeter protection for the most demanding security applications.
