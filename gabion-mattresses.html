Product Information

Gabion mattresses, also known as reno mattresses, are thin, flat wire mesh units designed specifically for erosion protection on riverbeds, channel bottoms, and sloped surfaces. Their low-profile geometry allows them to conform to irregular terrain while providing a stable, erosion-resistant foundation.

Manufactured from welded steel wire with galvanized or PVC coated finishes, gabion mattresses offer excellent flexibility and durability in hydraulic environments. The permeable structure allows water to flow through while the stone fill provides mass and stability against scouring forces.

Applications

Gabion mattresses are extensively used for channel lining, riverbank protection, scour protection around bridge piers and abutments, coastal defense structures, and outlet protection for culverts and drainage systems. Their flexibility and hydraulic performance make them the preferred choice for water-related erosion control projects.
