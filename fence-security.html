Engineered for Maximum Perimeter Protection

Security fence is a high-strength perimeter fencing system (heights 2–6 m, Y-post 40×40 to 75×75 mm) integrating anti-climb mesh panels—chain link, 3D welded, or 358 mesh—with barbed wire or razor wire toppings, designed for airports, military bases, prisons, and critical infrastructure. Kestrel Metal manufactures it from low-carbon steel with hot-dip galvanized (min. 270 g/m² zinc) or PVC coated finish, ISO 9001-certified production, FOB Tianjin, 15–25 day lead time.

Panel heights range from 2 m to 6 m with customizable mesh sizes (25×76 mm for 358 mesh, 50×200 mm for 3D panels) and wire diameters (3–6 mm). Compatible with CCTV mounting brackets, intrusion detection sensors, and electric fence energizers for integrated security solutions.

  • Anti-climb chain link, 3D, or 358 mesh

  • Razor / barbed wire topping options

Security Fence Features

Engineered for maximum perimeter protection with advanced anti-climb, weather-resistant, and integrated security capabilities.

Anti-Climb Design

Engineered with tight mesh openings, pointed post tops, and optional razor wire extensions to prevent unauthorized climbing. The Y-post and V-mesh configurations eliminate footholds, making it virtually impossible to scale without specialized equipment.

Maximum Perimeter Protection

Provides complete perimeter security for critical infrastructure. Available in heights up to 6m with integrated barbed wire or razor wire toppings, creating a multi-layered security barrier that deters, delays, and detects intrusion attempts.

Weather Resistance

Manufactured with hot-dip galvanized steel and optional PVC coating for superior corrosion resistance. Designed to withstand extreme temperatures, heavy rainfall, high humidity, and UV exposure with a service life of 20+ years.

Quick Installation

Pre-fabricated panels and modular post systems enable rapid deployment. Panels bolt directly to posts without welding on-site, reducing installation time by up to 50% compared to traditional fencing methods.

Customizable Configurations

Available in multiple mesh sizes, wire diameters, panel heights, and coating options. Post types include Y-post, square post, and round post to match specific security requirements and site conditions.

Integrated Security Systems

Compatible with CCTV cameras, intrusion detection sensors, fiber optic alarm cables, and access control systems. The fence structure provides ideal mounting points for surveillance and detection equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about security fence selection, installation, and perimeter protection.

What is the standard height for a security fence?
Security fence panels are typically available in heights from 2m to 6m. Standard configurations include 2.0m, 2.4m, 3.0m, 4.0m, and 6.0m depending on the site's security requirement. Airport, military, and prison perimeters commonly use panels of 3m or higher, often combined with razor wire toppings to extend the effective deterrent height.
How long does a security fence last?
A hot-dip galvanized security fence can last 20 to 50 years depending on coating thickness and environment. Hot-dip galvanized (HDG) systems typically last 20–40+ years, while 358 and V-mesh security panels with an additional PVC coating can extend service life further, especially in coastal or industrial atmospheres. Regular inspection and maintenance help maximize the lifespan of the entire perimeter system.
What is the difference between 358 mesh and standard security panels?
358 security mesh uses a very tight aperture of 12.7×76.2mm with thicker wire, making it effectively impossible to cut, climb, or expand with standard tools. V-mesh and chain link security panels offer larger apertures but remain anti-climb. 358 is specified for prisons, military, and government facilities requiring the highest containment and penetration-delay performance.
How much does a security fence cost?
Security fence costs are configuration-dependent and vary by mesh type, panel height, steel grade, coating, post system, and added toppings. As a general guide, chain link security fencing is the most economical, while 358 mesh and welded 3D panels command a premium for their higher security performance. Razor wire toppings, gate systems, and CCTV integration further affect total cost. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your perimeter length and security grade.
What is the best topping for a security fence?
The best topping depends on the required level of deterrence. Barbed wire is the most economical and is common for industrial perimeters. Razor wire offers a much higher deterrence level and is preferred for airports, military sites, and prisons. Installation options include single or double strand on straight arms, or a V-shaped cross-arm configuration. For extreme risk sites, concertina razor coils can be used to create a formidable secondary barrier.
What is the advantage of Y-shaped posts?
Y-shaped (Y-post) terminal posts distribute mesh tension across a wider base, providing greater structural rigidity and resistance to climbing attempts. The Y-profile design reduces the risk of anchor lifting and offers a stronger anchor point for toppings and tension hardware compared to square posts. Y-posts are a signature feature of high-security airport and military fencing systems.
Can a security fence integrate with CCTV and alarms?
Yes. Security fence systems are designed to integrate seamlessly with CCTV cameras, intrusion detection sensors, fiber-optic alarm cables, perimeter monitoring systems, and access control. Panels and Y-posts provide stable mounting points for camera brackets, and the mesh offers an ideal cable route for sensors and detection cables. This makes security fence a foundation for a complete electronic perimeter protection solution.
What steel grades are used for security fencing?
KESTREL METAL manufactures security fencing from high-quality low-carbon structural steel in grades Q195, Q235, and Q345. Q235 is the most common choice for panels and posts, offering a good balance of strength and formability. Q345 provides higher yield strength for heavy-duty or tall installations. All steel is supplied with hot-dip galvanized or PVC coated surface protection.
