What We Offer

Resources for Your Design Process

01

CAD & BIM Library

Access our comprehensive digital library of 2D CAD details and 3D BIM objects for all standard and custom wire mesh products. Our models are available in AutoCAD, Revit, SketchUp, and Rhino formats, pre-configured with accurate dimensions, material properties, and finish options for seamless integration into your project files.

02

Physical Sample Programs

Order full-size mesh samples, finish swatches, and material comparison kits to evaluate texture, transparency, color, and weight firsthand. Our sample program includes standard catalog items as well as custom samples produced to match your specific design parameters for client presentations and mock-ups.

03

Specification Assistance

Our technical team helps you write accurate, performance-based specifications for wire mesh and fencing products. We provide editable specification sections formatted for CSI MasterFormat, including technical data, testing requirements, installation methods, and warranty information ready to drop into your project manual.

04

Design Development Consultation

Work directly with our product engineers during schematic design and design development phases. We review your concepts, recommend suitable mesh types and materials, provide structural guidance for attachment systems, and help you optimize your design for manufacturability without compromising aesthetics.

Our Process

How We Support Your Design

1

Initial Consultation

Schedule a call or meeting with our design services team to discuss your project vision, performance requirements, timeline, and any specific design challenges you are working through.

2

Resource Delivery

We provide relevant CAD files, BIM models, material samples, and reference images of comparable completed projects to support your design development and client presentations.

3

Design Review & Refinement

Our engineers review your developing design, provide feedback on technical feasibility, suggest material and finish alternatives, and help resolve detailing questions for attachment and support systems.

4

Specification & Procurement

We deliver finalized specification sections, pricing guidance, and lead time estimates. When your project moves to construction, we transition seamlessly into manufacturing and delivery coordination.
