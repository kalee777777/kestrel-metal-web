Product Overview

Flexible, Permeable, Built to Last

Woven gabion boxes (gabion baskets) are made from hexagonal double-twisted woven wire mesh, filled with natural stones to create flexible, permeable structures for erosion control, retaining walls, river training, and landscaping applications.

The double-twist weaving technique prevents unravelling if a single wire breaks, providing superior flexibility that allows the structure to deform under load without losing integrity — ideal for dynamic environments like riverbanks and slopes.

  • Hexagonal double-twisted woven construction
  • High-tensile steel wire 350–550 MPa
  • Hot-dip galvanized, Galfan & PVC coated options
  • Flexible, self-locking mesh that resists unravelling
  • Eco-friendly, permeable and geotextile backed
  • Complies with ASTM A975 & BS EN 10223

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about woven gabion boxes.

What is a hexagonal double-twisted woven gabion?
A hexagonal double-twisted woven gabion is a wire mesh container made by twisting pairs of wires in a hexagonal pattern. The double-twist design means each wire is twisted around two adjacent wires, creating a self-locking mesh that prevents unravelling if a single wire breaks. This provides superior flexibility compared to welded gabions, allowing the structure to deform under load without losing integrity, making it ideal for dynamic environments like riverbanks and slopes.
What type of stone should I use to fill woven gabion boxes?
Woven gabion boxes should be filled with clean, hard, durable stone that is well-graded. The recommended stone size is typically 100mm to 250mm (4-10 inches), or 1.5 to 2 times the mesh opening size. The stones should be angular rather than rounded for better interlocking. Common materials include granite, basalt, limestone, and river rock. Hand-placing larger stones on exterior faces creates a neat appearance.
How are woven gabion boxes installed?
Installation involves several steps: 1) Prepare a level foundation surface, 2) Unfold the pre-assembled gabion panels and connect edges using spiral binders or lacing wire, 3) Place the assembled gabion in position, 4) Fill with stone in layers of 200-300mm, hand-placing larger stones on exterior faces, 5) Add internal diaphragm bracing for taller structures, 6) Close the lid and secure with spiral binders. The flexible nature of woven gabions allows them to conform to uneven terrain without special foundation work.
What is the expected lifespan of a woven gabion box?
The lifespan of woven gabion boxes depends on the coating type and environmental conditions. Hot-dip galvanized gabions typically last 20-40 years, while Galfan-coated gabions can last 30-50+ years. PVC-coated gabions offer the longest service life of 40-60+ years, especially in harsh or coastal environments. The double-twist woven construction provides additional longevity as the mesh maintains integrity even if individual wires are damaged over time.
Can woven gabion boxes be customized to specific sizes?
Yes, Kestrel Metal offers custom woven gabion box manufacturing to meet specific project requirements. We can customize dimensions, mesh opening sizes (typically 60x80mm, 80x100mm, or 100x120mm), wire diameters, and coating types. Custom sizes are particularly useful for architectural features, unique terrain conditions, or projects requiring non-standard configurations. Minimum order quantities may apply for custom specifications.
What standards do woven gabion boxes comply with?
Our woven gabion boxes are manufactured to comply with major international standards including BS EN 10223-3 (European standard for steel wire gabions and gabion mattresses), ASTM A975 (American standard for gabions and gabion mats), and BS EN 10244-2. All products undergo rigorous quality control testing including tensile strength, zinc coating weight, mesh opening dimensions, and twist integrity testing.
