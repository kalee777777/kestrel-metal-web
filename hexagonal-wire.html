Product Overview

Flexible Hexagonal Mesh, Woven for Reliability

Hexagonal wire netting is manufactured by twisting low-carbon steel wires into a distinctive hexagonal pattern. Its flexible, lightweight construction conforms easily to uneven ground and curved boundaries, making it one of the most versatile mesh products for fencing and protection.

Each weave type is produced with automated machinery to keep mesh openings and wire tension consistent across every roll. Choose the finish that best matches your environment — electro or hot-dip galvanized for economical corrosion resistance, PVC coated for longer service and color options, or stainless steel for demanding marine and chemical applications.

Why Hexagonal

Hexagonal Wire Netting Benefits

Product Information

Hexagonal wire netting is manufactured from low carbon steel wire with a zinc coating applied through either hot-dip galvanizing or electro galvanizing. The galvanized coating provides excellent protection against rust and corrosion, making it ideal for outdoor applications.

Available in both normal twist and reverse twist weaving patterns. Reverse twist offers greater structural stability as adjacent wires are twisted in opposite directions, preventing the mesh from unraveling if a single wire is cut. Normal twist is more economical and suitable for general-purpose applications.
