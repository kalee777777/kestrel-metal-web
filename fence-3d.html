Product Overview

Engineered for Security,
Designed for Aesthetics

The 3D wire panel fence is one of the most popular security fencing solutions worldwide, featuring cold-bend formed V-profile panels that provide exceptional structural rigidity without solder joints. Each panel is manufactured from high-quality low-carbon steel wire, welded at every intersection using automated precision equipment.

Available with hot-dip galvanization or dual-layer PVC coating for superior corrosion resistance, the unique 3D design enhances visual aesthetics while discouraging climbing — making it ideal for schools, parks, residential communities, industrial zones, and transportation infrastructure.

  • V-profile bends for rigidity
  • Welded at every intersection
  • Anti-climb mesh options
  • Hot-dip galvanized + PVC coated
  • 8 panel heights available
  • Custom RAL colors on request

Post Types

Fence Post Options

Choose the right post type for your fencing project. Each post is available in galvanized or PVC coated finish to match your panels.

Round Post

Ø48 / Ø60 mm

The most common and economical choice for general fencing applications. Thickness 1.5mm or 2.0mm, available in galvanized or PVC coated finish to match panel color.

Square Post

50×50 / 60×60 mm

Clean modern appearance with excellent structural stability. Ideal for architectural and premium residential projects where aesthetics matter.

Rectangular Post

40×60 to 80×100 mm

Enhanced rigidity for high-security and heavy-duty applications. Larger cross-section provides superior wind resistance and impact resistance.
