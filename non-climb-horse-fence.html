Product Information

Non-Climb Horse Fence features a patented 2"×4" mesh pattern that is specifically designed to prevent horse hooves from getting stuck or caught in the fence. The rigid mesh structure eliminates footholds, making it the safest fencing option for horse enclosures and paddocks.

The fixed knot construction ensures the mesh maintains its shape and integrity even under the weight and pressure of horses leaning or pushing against it. The 2"×4" opening is too small for a horse's hoof to pass through while still allowing excellent visibility for both horse and rider.

Benefits

  • ✓2"×4" mesh prevents hoof entrapment and injury
  • ✓No footholds — horses cannot climb the fence
  • ✓Galvanized steel for long-lasting durability
  • ✓Clear sight lines reduce horse anxiety
  • ✓Rigid structure maintains shape over time
  • ✓Easy installation on standard fence posts

Applications

Ideal for horse paddocks, riding arenas, equestrian centers, and breeding farms. The non-climb design is essential for safety when fencing horses, as traditional field fence can pose a risk of hoof entrapment.
