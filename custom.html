From custom mesh apertures and wire diameters to fully branded OEM runs, Kestrel Metal engineers products precisely to your drawing, specification and application.

What We Offer

Custom Capabilities

Flexible manufacturing across our entire wire mesh and fencing range, engineered around your exact project requirements from initial enquiry to final dispatch.

Custom Sizes & Specifications

Produce custom dimensions, aperture sizes, wire diameters and coating specifications to match your blueprint. Whether you need a non-standard panel width, a finer mesh opening or a specific zinc or PVC layer thickness, our production lines are tooled to accommodate bespoke parameters.

OEM & Private Label

Manufacture and package under your own brand for OEM and private label programmes. We handle custom branding, retail-ready packaging and documentation, so you can bring a complete, market-ready product line to your customers without building production capacity.

What We Can Customise

Customisation Options

From raw wire parameters to finished panel dimensions, nearly every production variable can be tailored to your needs.

Wire Diameter

Choose custom wire gauges across a wide range, from fine filter-grade strands to heavy structural wire, matched to your required strength and service life.

Mesh & Aperture

Specify custom mesh openings and weave patterns — square, rectangular, hexagonal or plain/twill weave — to achieve the exact screening or security performance.

Coating Customisation

Select from hot-dip galvanizing, PVC, epoxy or other protective coatings with custom layer thickness and colour options to extend corrosion resistance.

Panel Size

Produce welded mesh panels and fence rolls in custom lengths, heights and diameters to fit your construction site and installation layout precisely.

Cut-to-Size

Precision cut-hill and cut panels trimmed to exact measurements, minimising on-site waste and installation time for any project footprint.

Stainless & Galvanized

Choose from low-carbon galvanized, high-tensile, or full stainless steel grades (304/316) for corrosion resistance suited to coastal, food and industrial applications.
