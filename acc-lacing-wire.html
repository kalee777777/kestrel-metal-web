Assembly Instructions

Step-by-step guide for gabion box assembly using lacing wire

1Prepare Materials

Prepare all needed gabions, spiral wires, lacing wires and tools. Unfold and remove each gabion onto a flat hard surface.

2Erect Each Section

Form an open box by erecting the sides, ends and diaphragms vertically.

3Secure Panels with Lacing Wire

Joint the edges of gabion and diaphragms using lacing wire. Pull each loop tightly and secure the end with a loop and twist.

4Install Stiffeners

At 1/3 and 2/3 depth of fill, place stiffeners from front to back to prevent local deformation.

5Fill the Stones

Fill gabions with hard, durable stones. Place stones manually to minimize voids and ensure a neat appearance.

6Cover Lid with Lacing Wire

Place the top lid panel and secure with lacing wire around all edges, ensuring a tight and uniform closure.
