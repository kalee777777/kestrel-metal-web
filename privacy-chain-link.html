Product Information

Privacy chain link fencing combines the strength and security of traditional chain link mesh with factory-inserted double-wall privacy slats. The slats are woven through the diamond mesh during manufacturing, providing approximately 75% privacy and wind load reduction without on-site insertion.

Benefits

  • ✓75% privacy factor — effective visual screening
  • ✓Factory-inserted double-wall slats — saves installation labor
  • ✓Maintains full strength and durability of chain link
  • ✓Wind load reduction for improved stability
  • ✓Multiple color options to match surroundings
  • ✓Available up to 12' high in 25' rolls

Applications

Residential backyard enclosures, swimming pool areas, commercial property screening, industrial facility perimeters, school playgrounds, tennis courts, dog kennels.
