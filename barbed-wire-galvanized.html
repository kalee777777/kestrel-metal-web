Product Information

Galvanized barbed wire is made of high-quality low carbon steel wire that has been coated with a layer of zinc through either hot-dip galvanizing or electro galvanizing process. The zinc coating provides excellent protection against rust and corrosion, significantly extending the service life of the wire in outdoor environments.

Hot-dip galvanized barbed wire offers a thicker zinc coating (typically 40–270 g/m²) and longer service life, while electro galvanized barbed wire provides a smoother, brighter surface finish with a thinner coating (typically 8–15 g/m²).

Frequently Asked Questions

What wire gauges are available for galvanized barbed wire? +
Kestrel Metal produces galvanized barbed wire in wire gauges from 12 to 15.5 BWG. The most popular sizes are 12 BWG (2.7mm) and 14 BWG (2.0mm) for general fencing, and 12.5 BWG for high-security applications. Thicker wire (lower gauge number) provides greater strength and durability for perimeter security.
What barb spacing options do you offer? +
We manufacture barbed wire with barb spacings of 3 inches (75mm), 4 inches (100mm), 5 inches (125mm), and 6 inches (150mm). Closer barb spacing (3-4 inches) is recommended for high-security perimeters such as prisons and military installations. Wider spacing (5-6 inches) is suitable for agricultural fencing where cost efficiency is important.
What is the minimum order quantity for barbed wire? +
Our standard MOQ is 50 rolls (approximately 2,500 meters for standard 500m rolls). For larger orders of 500+ rolls, we offer significant volume discounts. We also support trial orders of 20 rolls for new customers to evaluate quality before committing to bulk purchase.
Is your barbed wire hot-dip or electro galvanized? +
We offer both options. Hot-dip galvanized barbed wire has a zinc coating of 40-270 g/m² and is recommended for outdoor fencing requiring 15-20+ years of corrosion resistance. Electro galvanized wire has a lighter coating and is suitable for temporary fencing or indoor applications. Hot-dip galvanized is our most popular option for agricultural and security fencing.
What are the standard roll lengths and weights? +
Standard roll lengths are 100m, 250m, 500m, and 1000m. Weight varies by wire gauge and barb spacing. For example, a 500m roll of 12 BWG wire with 4-inch barb spacing weighs approximately 32 kg. We can customize roll length and weight to match your project specifications and shipping requirements.
Can galvanized barbed wire be combined with other fencing? +
Absolutely. Galvanized barbed wire is commonly used as a topping for chain link fences, welded wire mesh fences, and palisade fences. It is also used with Y-posts or angle iron posts for standalone perimeter fencing. For enhanced security, barbed wire can be combined with razor wire concertina coils.
