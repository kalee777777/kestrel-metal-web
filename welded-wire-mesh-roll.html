Product Overview

Continuous Welded Mesh for Efficient Installation

Welded wire mesh rolls are produced by welding intersecting wires at each crossing point using electric resistance welding, then wound into continuous rolls for easy transportation and installation. The welded construction provides excellent rigidity along the length of the roll while maintaining flexibility across the width.

These rolls are widely used for fencing, reinforcement, screening, and protection applications where long, continuous runs of wire mesh are required. The roll format eliminates the need for joining multiple panels, saving time and labor during installation.

  • Electric resistance welded intersections for maximum strength
  • Continuous roll format for long, uninterrupted runs
  • Available in galvanized, PVC coated & stainless steel
  • Mesh openings from 50×50 mm to 150×300 mm
  • Wire diameters from 2.5 mm to 6.0 mm
  • Standard roll lengths of 25m, 50m, and 100m

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about welded wire mesh rolls.

What are the standard roll sizes available?
Standard welded wire mesh rolls are available in widths ranging from 0.9m to 2.0m and lengths of 25m, 50m, and 100m. The most common sizes are 1.0m x 50m and 1.2m x 50m. Custom roll widths and lengths can be manufactured to meet specific project requirements depending on the quantity ordered.
How is welded wire mesh roll different from woven wire mesh roll?
Welded wire mesh roll is made by electric resistance welding at each wire intersection, creating a rigid mesh that maintains its shape. Woven wire mesh roll is produced by interlacing wires, resulting in a more flexible material. Welded mesh offers superior tensile strength and dimensional precision, while woven mesh is more flexible and easier to bend around corners or curved surfaces.
What is the typical weight of a welded wire mesh roll?
The weight of a welded wire mesh roll depends on wire diameter, mesh opening, roll width and length. A typical 1.0m x 50m roll with 4.0mm wire and 50mm x 100mm mesh weighs approximately 15-20 kg. Larger rolls with thicker wire can weigh up to 50-100 kg. Weights for specific configurations are available on request.
How do I install a welded wire mesh roll?
Installation steps for welded wire mesh rolls: 1) Mark the fence line and post positions, 2) Install posts at recommended spacing (typically 2.0-2.5m for welded mesh), 3) Unroll the mesh along the fence line, 4) Attach the mesh to posts using appropriate clips or tie wire, 5) Keep the mesh taut to maintain tension, 6) Secure the end post and add post caps. Welded mesh rolls are faster to install than panels for long continuous runs.
What surface treatment should I choose for my application?
The choice of surface treatment depends on your environment and budget: Galvanized is the most cost-effective for general outdoor use, providing 15-20 years of rust protection. PVC coated offers superior corrosion resistance (25-30 years) and comes in various colors for aesthetic applications. Stainless steel (304/316) is recommended for coastal, chemical, or food processing environments, providing the highest level of corrosion resistance.
