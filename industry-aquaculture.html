Industry Challenges

Solving Aquaculture's Toughest Problems

We understand the unique challenges aquaculture professionals face — and we engineer solutions to address each one.

Marine Corrosion

Continuous exposure to saltwater and harsh marine environments rapidly degrades untreated steel, shortening the service life of aquaculture mesh products.

Hot-dip galvanized and PVC-coated marine-grade wire delivers long-term corrosion protection, with 316L stainless options for the most aggressive saltwater and brackish conditions.

Predator Protection

Birds, mammals, and other predators threaten fish stocks and shrimp populations, causing significant losses for aquaculture operations.

Strong mesh enclosures and anti-predator fencing with heavy-gauge wire and optimized aperture sizing physically deter predators while maintaining water flow and stock safety.

Water Flow & Oxygen

Maintaining proper water circulation and dissolved oxygen levels is critical — mesh that restricts flow can harm fish health and growth rates.

Optimized mesh aperture sizes balance predator exclusion, stock containment, and water exchange to maintain the circulation and oxygen transfer required for healthy aquaculture systems.

Biofouling Resistance

Algae, barnacles, and other organisms build up on submerged mesh, reducing water flow and increasing maintenance burdens for aquaculture operators.

Smooth PVC coating reduces biofouling adhesion, making cleaning easier and extending intervals between maintenance while protecting the underlying wire from corrosive attack.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What coating is best for marine aquaculture environments?
For saltwater aquaculture, we recommend hot-dip galvanized with PVC coating, or 316L stainless steel for maximum corrosion resistance. The PVC coating also reduces biofouling adhesion, making cage cleaning easier and extending service life.
What mesh size is appropriate for fish cages?
Mesh size depends on fish species and growth stage. Fingerling cages typically use 6-13mm mesh, while grow-out cages use 25-50mm mesh. The mesh must balance fish containment with water flow for oxygen exchange — we provide custom aperture sizes for your specific species.
Can your mesh withstand predator attacks?
Yes. Our fish cage netting is manufactured from high-tensile galvanized or stainless steel wire that resists tearing by birds, mammals, and other aquatic predators. For high-risk areas, we recommend heavier wire gauges and double-layer designs.
Do you supply water intake screening for aquaculture facilities?
Yes. We manufacture stainless steel screens for water intake systems that prevent fish entry and debris accumulation while maintaining required flow rates. These screens meet environmental regulations for aquatic organism exclusion at pump intakes.
