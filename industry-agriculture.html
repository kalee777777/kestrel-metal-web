Industry Challenges

Solving Agriculture's Toughest Problems

We understand the unique challenges farmers and livestock operators face — and we engineer solutions to address each one.

Predator & Wildlife Control

Coyotes, foxes, wild dogs, and other predators threaten livestock and poultry, causing significant losses for farm operations.

High-tensile fixed-knot fencing with narrow bottom apertures and reinforced verticals creates an effective predator barrier that resists digging, climbing, and impact from large animals.

Weather & UV Degradation

Constant exposure to sunlight, rain, humidity, and temperature cycling degrades untreated steel fencing, requiring frequent replacement.

Zinc-aluminum and PVC coating provide superior corrosion and UV resistance, extending fence service life to 30+ years even in coastal, tropical, and high-rainfall agricultural environments.

Animal Safety

Sharp wire ends and poorly designed knots can injure valuable livestock, leading to veterinary costs and stressed animals.

Smooth wire surfaces and proper knot designs — including hinge-joint and fixed-knot construction — eliminate sharp protrusions while maintaining fence strength and animal containment.

Large Area Coverage

Farms and ranches require miles of perimeter and internal fencing — making cost-effective, durable solutions critical to profitability.

High-tensile wire with wider post spacing reduces material and labor costs per kilometer while maintaining fence integrity, delivering economical perimeter coverage for large agricultural properties.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What coating is best for agricultural fencing?
For most farm applications, hot-dip galvanized (ASTM A641) provides excellent corrosion resistance. In coastal or high-humidity areas, we recommend zinc-aluminum coating or PVC-coated wire for extended service life of 30+ years.
How far apart should fence posts be spaced?
Post spacing depends on fence type and terrain. Field fence typically uses 3-5 meter spacing on flat ground, reducing to 2-3 meters on hilly terrain. High-tensile fencing allows wider spacing up to 6 meters.
Can your fencing withstand livestock pressure?
Yes. Our high-tensile fixed-knot and hinge-joint fencing is engineered to withstand cattle and horse pressure. Fixed-knot fencing offers superior vertical and horizontal strength compared to traditional hinge-joint designs.
Do you offer predator-proof fencing options?
Yes. We manufacture wildlife exclusion fencing with smaller bottom apertures and apron designs that prevent digging predators. These systems protect poultry, sheep, and other vulnerable livestock from coyotes, foxes, and other threats.
