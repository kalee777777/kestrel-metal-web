FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What fencing is best for residential gardens?
For garden fencing, PVC-coated welded mesh or chain link in green or black provides an attractive, durable boundary. Heights of 1.2-1.8 meters are typical. For decorative appeal, consider powder-coated panels in colors matching your landscape.
Do you offer colored fencing options?
Yes. We offer PVC-coated and powder-coated fencing in green, black, brown, white, and custom colors. Colored coatings blend with landscaping while providing additional corrosion protection for 15-20+ years of outdoor service.
What mesh size keeps insects out of windows?
Standard insect window screens use 18x16 mesh count with 0.25mm wire diameter. For finer pest control (no-see-ums, gnats), 20x20 mesh is recommended. We supply both fiberglass and stainless steel window screen options.
Is your fencing safe for children and pets?
Yes. Our residential fencing features smooth wire surfaces with no sharp edges. Enclosure mesh for pet runs and rabbit cages uses appropriately sized apertures to prevent injury while containing animals safely.
