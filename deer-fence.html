Product Information

Deer Fence is engineered to stand 1.8m to 2.4m tall — well above the maximum jumping height of most deer species. The rigid fixed-knot construction ensures the fence maintains its height and integrity even when deer attempt to push through or lean against it.

Beyond height, the close wire spacing prevents deer from seeing through and attempting to squeeze or push through the fence. The heavy-duty galvanized steel construction withstands the outdoor elements and provides long-lasting protection for crops, gardens, and orchards.

Applications

Essential for crop protection on farms, orchards, vineyards, and nursery operations. Also ideal for protecting residential gardens, landscaping, and ornamental plants from deer browsing. Can be combined with electric fencing for enhanced wildlife exclusion.
