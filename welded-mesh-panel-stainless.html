Product Information

Stainless steel welded wire mesh panels are manufactured from high-quality AISI 304 or 316 stainless steel wire, welded at each intersection using TIG welding technology. This construction provides exceptional corrosion resistance and structural integrity.

AISI 304 grade offers good corrosion resistance for general applications, while AISI 316 provides superior resistance to marine environments and chemical exposure.

Benefits

  • ✓Ultimate corrosion and rust resistance for harsh environments.
  • ✓Marine-grade AISI 316 available for coastal installations.
  • ✓Food-safe and easy to clean for processing facilities.
  • ✓Attractive metallic finish for architectural projects.
  • ✓Long service life with minimal maintenance.
  • ✓Resistant to chemicals and cleaning agents.

Applications

Stainless steel welded wire mesh panels are ideal for marine and coastal environments, food processing facilities, pharmaceutical production, architectural façades, chemical plants, and high-end decorative projects.
