Product Information

PVC coated welded wire mesh panels combine the strength of welded steel mesh with the aesthetic appeal and corrosion protection of PVC coating. The PVC layer is applied after galvanizing, creating a dual protective barrier that extends the panel's service life significantly.

These panels are an excellent choice for applications where both performance and appearance matter, such as garden fencing, landscape borders, and architectural projects.

Benefits

  • ✓Superior corrosion resistance with dual galvanized + PVC protection.
  • ✓Decorative finish available in multiple colors.
  • ✓Weather-resistant for all outdoor climates.
  • ✓Rigid welded construction maintains shape.
  • ✓Non-toxic and UV-stabilized PVC coating.
  • ✓Easy to cut and install on site.

Applications

PVC coated welded wire mesh panels are ideal for garden fencing, landscape borders, playground enclosures, residential boundaries, sports field fencing, and decorative architectural applications.
