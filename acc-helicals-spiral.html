Product Information

Helicals and spiral wire are pre-formed spiral binders designed for rapid mechanical connection of gabion mesh panels. Unlike lacing wire, spiral wires are installed using a special tool, allowing for faster and more consistent assembly.

The spiral design provides strong interlocking between panels, ensuring secure connections that maintain structural integrity under load. Available in various sizes to match different gabion mesh openings.

Benefits

  • Rapid mechanical connection with tool installation
  • Strong interlocking between gabion panels
  • Consistent fastening quality
  • Corrosion resistant galvanized or PVC coating

Assembly Instructions

Step-by-step guide for gabion box assembly using helical & spiral wire

1Prepare Materials

Prepare gabions, spiral wires, lacing wires and tools on a flat surface.

2Erect Each Section

Form an open box by erecting the sides, ends and diaphragms vertically.

3Secure with Spiral Wires

Use spiral wire tool to install helical binders at panel edges for secure mechanical connection.

4Install Stiffeners

Place stiffeners at 1/3 and 2/3 fill depth to prevent deformation.

5Fill the Stones

Fill gabions with hard, durable stones, minimizing voids.

6Cover Lid with Spiral Wires

Place the top lid and secure with spiral wires around all edges.
