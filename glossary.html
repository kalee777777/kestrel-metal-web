Category 01

Fence Products

  • 3D Wire Panel FenceA security fencing system using V-profile cold-formed welded mesh panels with horizontal ribs for enhanced rigidity. Commonly used for industrial, military, and infrastructure perimeter protection.
  • Chain Link FenceAlso known as diamond mesh fence or cyclone fence. A woven wire fence made from galvanized or PVC-coated steel wire in a diamond pattern, offering versatility and cost-effectiveness for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.
  • Airport Fence (Y-Post Security Fence)A high-security fencing system using Y-profile steel posts and welded mesh panels, designed specifically for airport perimeters and other high-security zones.
  • Hinge Joint Fence (Field Fence)A traditional agricultural fence using a "live knot" (hinge joint) connection that provides excellent elasticity and impact resistance. Ideal for boundary fencing and livestock grazing enclosures per ASTM A116.
  • S-Knot FenceA specialized fencing product featuring an S-shaped knot that provides smooth, safe surfaces for horse and livestock enclosures.
  • Fixed Knot FenceA high-strength agricultural fence with fixed knots that lock horizontal and vertical wires securely, suitable for deer fencing and large animal containment.
  • Temporary Fence / Crowd Control BarrierInterlocking steel panels with weighted bases for rapid deployment at construction sites, events, and public gatherings.
  • Palisade FencingA high-security fencing system with vertical steel pales and pointed tops, commonly used for industrial facilities and government installations.
  • Welded Mesh FenceA fencing system using pre-fabricated welded wire mesh panels mounted on steel posts, offering quick installation and reliable perimeter security.
  • CrimpsSpecial wire deformations that allow the fence to expand and contract due to changing weather conditions. When installing Field Fence, pull the wire so that about half of the curve is pulled out of the wire.
  • Graduated SpacingA fence design where vertical wires are spaced closer together at the bottom and gradually increase in spacing toward the top, keeping small animals out without wasting material.
Category 02

Wire Mesh Products

  • Welded Wire MeshA metal grid made by spot-welding intersecting steel wires at right angles. Available in galvanized, PVC-coated, and stainless steel variants for fencing, construction, and industrial screening applications.
  • Hexagonal Wire Netting (Chicken Wire)A lightweight, hexagonal-pattern woven wire mesh, commonly known as chicken wire. Used for poultry enclosures, garden protection, and stucco reinforcement.
  • Window ScreenFine mesh made from stainless steel, fiberglass, or aluminum, designed to allow airflow while preventing insects from entering buildings.
  • V-Profile Welded Mesh PanelA welded mesh panel with V-shaped ribs pressed into the horizontal wires, providing enhanced structural rigidity and anti-climb properties.
  • Wedge Wire ScreenA precision-engineered screen with V-shaped wire profiles welded to support rods, used for water intake screening, dewatering, and particle classification.
  • Gabion MeshDouble-twisted hexagonal wire mesh used to create stone-filled baskets (gabions) for retaining walls, erosion control, and slope stabilization.
Category 03

Wire Products

  • Barbed WireA type of steel fencing wire with sharp barbs spaced at regular intervals. Available in single twist, double twist, and traditional twist configurations for agricultural and security fencing.
  • Razor WireAlso known as barbed tape. A high-security product made of sharp steel blades formed into a helical coil around a core wire. Available in BTO, CBT, and concertina configurations.
  • Concertina Razor WireRazor wire formed into large-diameter coils that expand like a spring, creating an effective physical barrier for military, prison, and border security applications.
  • BTC Barbed Tape Concertina (NATO-22)A military-grade razor wire product conforming to NATO-22 (MIL-AA-55522A) standard, featuring fish-hook blade profiles for maximum deterrence.
Category 04

Materials & Coatings

  • Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG)A corrosion protection process where steel is immersed in molten zinc, forming a metallurgically bonded zinc coating. Provides long-lasting protection per ASTM A123 and ISO 1461 standards.
  • Electro-GalvanizedA zinc coating applied by electroplating, producing a thinner and smoother finish compared to hot-dip galvanizing.
  • PVC CoatingA polyvinyl chloride plastic coating extruded over galvanized wire, providing additional corrosion resistance and color options for outdoor applications.
  • PE CoatingA polyethylene coating applied over galvanized wire for enhanced UV resistance and durability in agricultural and marine environments.
  • Stainless Steel 304An austenitic stainless steel alloy containing 18% chromium and 8% nickel, offering good corrosion resistance for general-purpose applications.
  • Stainless Steel 316 / 316LA molybdenum-bearing stainless steel alloy with superior chloride corrosion resistance, ideal for marine, chemical, and pharmaceutical applications.
  • Powder CoatingA dry finishing process where electrostatically charged powder is sprayed onto a surface and cured under heat, providing a durable and uniform protective layer.
  • Copper AlloyA metal alloy (typically copper-zinc or copper-nickel) with natural anti-fouling properties, used in premium aquaculture mesh applications.
  • Carbon Steel (Low Carbon Steel)A steel alloy with low carbon content (typically at most 0.22%), offering good weldability and formability for wire mesh and fencing products.
  • Low Carbon WireA type of wire that is thicker and more cost-effective, bending and flexing easily. Low carbon wire is ideal for temporary fencing or less demanding applications where flexibility is preferred over strength.
  • High Tensile WireA type of steel wire that is thin but strong and lightweight, with excellent tensile strength. Unlike low carbon wire, high tensile wire won't stretch out over time, making it ideal for permanent, long-lasting fence installations.
  • GalvanizationA corrosion protection process that coats steel with zinc to defend against rust. This protective layer helps reduce exposure to humidity, salt air, and other corrosive chemicals, extending the life of the fence.
Category 05

Technical Specifications

  • Wire DiameterThe thickness of individual wires in a mesh or fence product, measured in millimeters (mm) or gauge (ga). A key parameter determining product strength and weight.
  • Mesh Size / ApertureThe distance between adjacent parallel wires in a mesh, measured from center to center. Determines the mesh's filtering, screening, or containment capability.
  • Mesh CountThe number of openings per linear inch or per square inch. Higher mesh counts indicate finer mesh with smaller openings.
  • Tensile StrengthThe maximum stress a material can withstand while being stretched before breaking, measured in MPa or psi. Critical for fencing and structural applications.
  • Zinc Coating WeightThe mass of zinc coating per unit area (g/m square), indicating the thickness and quality of galvanized protection. Higher values provide longer corrosion life.
  • PorosityThe percentage of open area in a mesh or porous material, determining flow rate and screening capability.
  • Filtration RatingThe nominal or absolute particle size (in microns) that a filter element can reliably capture, indicating filtration precision.
  • Burst PressureThe maximum pressure differential a filter element can withstand before structural failure, a critical safety parameter for pressurized systems.
  • GaugeThe thickness of the wire. The higher the number, the smaller/thinner the wire. Low gauge refers to thicker wire, while high gauge wire refers to lighter weight wire.
Category 06

Fencing Components & Fittings

  • Loop CapA metal fitting used to connect the top rail to line posts in chain link fence systems, providing a secure and clean connection point.
  • Tension BandA galvanized steel band wrapped around terminal posts to distribute tension forces from the fence fabric evenly.
  • Tension BarA vertical bar woven through the chain link mesh end and attached to tension bands, used to stretch and secure the fence fabric.
  • Barb ArmA metal bracket attached to fence posts for mounting barbed wire strands above the main fence line.
  • End Post / Terminal PostA heavy-duty post installed at fence ends, corners, and gates, designed to withstand the full tension load of the fence fabric.
  • Line PostAn intermediate post installed between terminal posts to support the fence fabric at regular intervals.
  • Corner PostA post installed at fence corners, typically braced to resist directional tension forces from both adjacent fence runs.
  • Hog RingsMetal rings used to secure chain link fabric to the fence frame, providing a quick and reliable attachment method.
  • TurnbuckleA threaded tensioning device used to adjust and maintain wire tension in fence systems.
  • Post Cap / Dome CapA decorative and protective cover fitted to the top of fence posts to prevent water ingress.
  • Pull OutThe distance between the last vertical wire and the next post, typically ranging from 6 to 16 inches. This spacing allows the fence to be stretched tight and secured properly to the post.
Category 07

Manufacturing Processes

  • MIG & TIG WeldingMetal Inert Gas and Tungsten Inert Gas welding processes used to join metal components with high precision and structural integrity.
  • Wire DrawingA metalworking process that reduces wire diameter by pulling the wire through a series of progressively smaller dies.
  • Hot-Dip GalvanizingThe process of dipping fabricated steel products into a bath of molten zinc (typically 450 degrees Celsius) to form a corrosion-resistant zinc-iron alloy coating.
  • PVC Extrusion CoatingA process where molten PVC is extruded over galvanized wire through a die, forming a uniform plastic coating that bonds to the zinc surface.
  • SinteringA high-temperature process that bonds metal particles or wire mesh layers together through diffusion, creating porous materials with controlled permeability.
  • CNC CuttingComputer Numerical Controlled cutting using laser, plasma, or waterjet technology for precise metal component fabrication.
  • PassivationA chemical treatment for stainless steel that removes free iron from the surface and enhances the natural chromium oxide layer for improved corrosion resistance.
  • Powder CoatingAn electrostatic spray finishing process where dry powder is applied to a grounded metal surface and cured in an oven to form a hard, durable finish.
Category 08

Standards & Certifications

  • ASTM A123Standard specification for zinc (hot-dip galvanized) coatings on iron and steel products.
  • ASTM A116Standard specification for steel wire, zinc-coated (galvanized), for agricultural field fence.
  • BS EN 10244-2British/European standard for zinc or zinc alloy coatings on steel wire.
  • ISO 9001International standard for quality management systems, certifying consistent manufacturing and quality control processes.
  • ISO 1461International standard for hot dip galvanized coatings on fabricated iron and steel articles.
  • NATO-22 (MIL-AA-55522A)North Atlantic Treaty Organization military specification for barbed tape concertina, defining blade dimensions and performance requirements.
