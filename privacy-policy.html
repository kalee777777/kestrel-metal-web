1. Introduction

By accessing or using our Website, submitting inquiries, placing orders, or otherwise providing personal data to us, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, please discontinue use of our Website and do not provide us with your personal data.

3. Types of Data Collected

We collect and process different categories of personal data depending on the nature of your interaction with us. The types of data we may collect include the following:

3.1 Navigation Data

When you visit our Website, our IT systems may automatically collect certain technical information. This data is collected passively and does not require you to provide any information directly. Navigation data may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • Operating system and device type
  • Pages visited, visit duration, and navigation paths within the Website
  • Date and time of each access and session
  • Referring website or source URL
  • Geographic location (country and city level, based on IP address)
  • Screen resolution and device settings
  • Click patterns, scroll depth, and interaction events

This navigation data is used for statistical analysis, security monitoring, and improving the performance and usability of our Website. IP addresses are anonymized or pseudonymized where technically feasible.

3.2 Data Provided Voluntarily by the User

When you interact with us by submitting inquiries, requesting quotes, placing orders, subscribing to newsletters, or contacting our customer service team, you may voluntarily provide personal data. This may include:

  • Full name (first name and last name)

  • Telephone number (including country code)
  • Company or organization name
  • Job title or role
  • Mailing address and shipping address
  • Product inquiries, specifications, and project requirements
  • Messages, comments, or feedback submitted through contact forms
  • Payment and billing information (processed through secure third-party payment gateways)
  • Any other information you choose to provide in communications with us

Failure to provide certain mandatory data fields (marked with an asterisk on our forms) may result in our inability to process your inquiry, fulfill your order, or provide the requested services.

3.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Website uses cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze Website traffic, and deliver personalized content. 5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the GDPR and other applicable data protection laws, we process your personal data only when a valid legal basis applies. The legal bases on which we rely include:

5.1 Consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))

Where you have given us clear, informed, and freely given consent to process your personal data for specific purposes, such as receiving marketing communications or accepting non-essential cookies. You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

5.2 Performance of a Contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b))

Where processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party, or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract. This includes processing your data to fulfill purchase orders, deliver products, and provide contracted services.

5.3 Legitimate Interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f))

Where processing is necessary for the purposes of our legitimate interests or those of a third party, provided that such interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights and freedoms. We rely on legitimate interest for purposes such as website analytics, fraud prevention, direct marketing to existing customers (where permitted by law), and maintaining the security of our systems and services.

5.4 Legal Obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c))

Where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject. This includes retaining records for tax, accounting, customs, and regulatory compliance purposes as required by Chinese law and the laws of jurisdictions in which we operate.

5.5 Special Categories of Data

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, biometric data, or data concerning sexual orientation). If such data is inadvertently provided to us, we will delete it promptly and securely.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws and regulations. Our data retention practices are guided by the following principles:

  • Active Customer Data: Personal data relating to active business relationships, ongoing orders, and pending inquiries is retained for the duration of the business relationship and for a reasonable period thereafter to support warranty claims, after-sales service, and dispute resolution.
  • Contractual and Financial Records: Data related to orders, invoices, contracts, and financial transactions is retained for a minimum of five (5) years from the end of the relevant fiscal year, in accordance with Chinese tax and accounting regulations.
  • Marketing Data: If you have consented to receive marketing communications, your contact data will be retained until you withdraw your consent. Upon withdrawal, your data will be removed from our active marketing databases within thirty (30) days.
  • Website Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely for statistical purposes. Raw navigation data (including IP addresses) is retained for a maximum of twenty-six (26) months from the date of collection.
  • Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Data may be retained for longer periods if required by applicable law, regulation, or legal proceedings. In such cases, data will be retained only for the minimum period required and will be securely deleted once the legal obligation no longer applies.
  • Expired Data: When personal data is no longer required for the purposes described above, it is securely deleted or anonymized in accordance with our internal data management procedures.

8. Cookies Policy

8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better browsing experience, and supply information to the website owners. Cookies can be "persistent" (stored on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted automatically when you close your browser).

8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Our Website uses the following categories of cookies:

Cookie Category Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Essential for the Website to function properly, including session management, security verification, and shopping cart functionality. These cookies cannot be disabled. Session / Persistent
Performance / Analytics Collect anonymous information about how visitors use the Website, such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and error messages. This helps us improve Website performance and content. Tools include Google Analytics. Up to 26 months
Functionality Remember your preferences and settings (such as language preference, region, and display settings) to provide a more personalized experience. Up to 12 months
Targeting / Marketing Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. These cookies may be set by us or our advertising partners. Up to 24 months

8.3 Managing Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in the following ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings menu. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or delete cookies when you close your browser. Please refer to your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.
  • Cookie Consent Banner: When you first visit our Website, a cookie consent banner will appear, allowing you to accept or customize your cookie preferences. You may update your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our Website.
  • Opt-Out Tools: For third-party analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics), you may install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For advertising cookies, you may visit the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page or the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and user experience of our Website. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled, as they are essential for the operation of the Website.

9. Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect it against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security measures include, but are not limited to:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: Our Website uses Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption to protect data transmitted between your browser and our servers. All pages on our Website that collect or display personal data are served over HTTPS.
  • Access Controls: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who require it to perform their job duties. We implement role-based access controls, unique user accounts, and strong password policies to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Data Encryption: Sensitive personal data is encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption algorithms (such as AES-256) to protect it in the event of unauthorized physical access to our storage systems.
  • Network Security: Our IT infrastructure is protected by firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS), and regular vulnerability assessments. We monitor our systems for suspicious activity and respond promptly to potential security incidents.
  • Employee Training: Our employees and contractors receive regular training on data protection best practices, information security policies, and their obligations under applicable data protection laws.
  • Physical Security: Physical access to our premises and data centers is controlled through security measures including access cards, surveillance systems, and visitor management protocols.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security audits and assessments to identify and address potential vulnerabilities in our systems and processes.
  • Incident Response: We maintain a data breach incident response plan that defines procedures for detecting, containing, investigating, and reporting security incidents.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you acknowledge that you provide your personal data at your own risk.

10. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable data protection laws, you may be entitled to certain rights regarding your personal data. We are committed to facilitating the exercise of these rights. The rights available to you may include:

10.1 Right of Access (GDPR Article 15)

You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether we process your personal data and, if so, to access a copy of that data along with information about the purposes of processing, the categories of data concerned, the recipients or categories of recipients, the retention period, and the source of the data.

10.2 Right to Rectification (GDPR Article 16)

You have the right to request the correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you. We will take reasonable steps to update or amend your data promptly upon receiving your request and any necessary supporting information.

10.3 Right to Erasure / Right to Be Forgotten (GDPR Article 17)

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data where it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, where you have withdrawn your consent and there is no other legal basis for processing, where the data has been unlawfully processed, or where erasure is required by applicable law. Please note that this right is subject to certain exceptions, such as where retention is required for legal compliance, the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

10.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Article 18)

You have the right to request the restriction of processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as where you contest the accuracy of the data, where processing is unlawful but you prefer restriction over erasure, where we no longer need the data but you require it for legal claims, or where you have objected to processing pending verification of legitimate grounds.

10.5 Right to Data Portability (GDPR Article 20)

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (such as CSV or JSON), and to transmit that data to another data controller without hindrance, where processing is based on consent or contract and is carried out by automated means.

10.6 Right to Object (GDPR Article 21)

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. Where you object to processing for direct marketing, we will cease processing your data for such purposes without delay. Where you object to processing based on legitimate interests, we will cease processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms.

10.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produce legal effects or similarly significant effects concerning you. We do not currently engage in fully automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

10.8 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal. You may withdraw consent by using the unsubscribe mechanism in our communications, updating your cookie preferences, or contacting us directly.

10.9 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your jurisdiction if you believe that the processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection laws. In the European Union, you may contact the data protection authority of your Member State of residence or the authority where you believe a breach has occurred. Relevant contact details for supervisory authorities can be found on the website of the European Data Protection Board (EDPB).

10.10 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer using the contact details provided in Section 13. We will respond to your request within thirty (30) days of receipt. In certain circumstances, we may extend this period by a further sixty (60) days where necessary, in which case we will notify you of the extension and the reasons for the delay. We may request verification of your identity before processing your request to ensure the security of your personal data.

11. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, we will comply with our obligations under GDPR Article 33 and any other applicable data protection laws. Our breach notification procedures include:

  • Notification to Supervisory Authorities: Where a breach is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons. Where the seventy-two-hour deadline cannot be met, we will provide reasons for the delay along with the notification.
  • Notification to Affected Individuals: Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, we will communicate the breach to the affected data subjects without undue delay. The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the name and contact details of our DPO, the likely consequences of the breach, and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach, including measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects.
  • Internal Investigation: Upon detection of a suspected breach, we will promptly initiate an internal investigation to assess the nature, scope, and impact of the incident, contain the breach, and implement remedial measures to prevent recurrence.
  • Documentation: We will maintain a record of all personal data breaches, including the facts relating to the breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken, regardless of whether notification to a supervisory authority or affected individuals is required.
