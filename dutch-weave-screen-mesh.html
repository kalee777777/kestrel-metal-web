Dutch Weave Construction

Dutch weave is a specialized wire weave where the warp and weft wires have different diameters. In PDW (Plain Dutch Weave), the thicker warp wires run straight while the thinner weft wires are woven over and under, creating a dense mesh structure. In TDW (Twill Dutch Weave), the weft wires follow a twill pattern, producing even finer openings for ultra-fine filtration.

This unique construction allows Dutch weave mesh to achieve much smaller and more uniform openings compared to standard plain or twill weaves of equivalent mesh counts, making it ideal for applications requiring precise fine particle separation.
