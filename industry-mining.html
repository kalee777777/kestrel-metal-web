FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What material is best for high-wear screening applications?
High-manganese steel (Hadfield steel) offers the best wear resistance for primary crushing and high-impact screening. For secondary screening, high-carbon steel or stainless steel 304/316 may be more cost-effective depending on the abrasive characteristics of your feed material.
Can you manufacture custom screen panel sizes?
Yes. We fabricate screen panels to match any crusher, vibrating screen, or trommel dimensions. Provide your equipment specifications or drawings and we will manufacture panels with exact aperture sizes, wire diameters, and mounting configurations.
How quickly can you supply replacement screen panels?
Standard panel sizes are typically in stock and ship within 5-7 days. Custom panels require 10-20 days depending on material availability and quantity. We offer expedited production for emergency replacements to minimize downtime.
Do your security fencing systems meet mine site regulations?
Yes. Our heavy-duty security fencing features anti-climb and anti-cut mesh designs that meet MSHA and international mine site safety regulations. We can provide documentation and compliance certificates for your safety audits.
