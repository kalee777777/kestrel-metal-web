What We Offer

Estimating & Drafting Services

01

Material Quantity Takeoffs

Our estimators analyze your architectural plans, site surveys, and project specifications to produce comprehensive material quantity breakdowns. We calculate linear footage of fencing, square footage of mesh panels, quantities of posts, fittings, gates, and accessories — all organized by zone, phase, or area to align with your project schedule and bidding structure.

02

Shop Drawing Preparation

We create detailed shop drawings for every fencing and mesh installation, showing exact panel dimensions, post spacing, gate openings, foundation details, and attachment hardware. Each drawing set is reviewed against the contract documents and submitted for approval with a clear transmittal of any field conditions or deviations noted during review.

03

Bill of Materials & Pricing

Alongside our takeoff reports, we provide itemized bills of materials with current pricing for all wire mesh and fencing components. This includes standard items from our catalog as well as custom-fabricated pieces, allowing you to build accurate budgets and compare options before placing orders.

04

As-Built & Record Drawings

After installation, we produce as-built drawings that document the actual field conditions, including any modifications made during construction. These record drawings serve as a permanent reference for facility management, future expansions, and maintenance planning.

Our Process

How We Deliver Accurate Documentation

1

Plan Review

Submit your project drawings, specifications, and site plans. Our team reviews all documents to understand the full scope, identify special conditions, and clarify any ambiguities before beginning the takeoff.

2

Quantity Analysis

We perform a detailed measurement of all wire mesh, fencing, and accessory quantities using your plans. Each item is categorized, measured, and cross-referenced against the project specifications for accuracy.

3

Drafting & Review

Shop drawings are prepared in AutoCAD showing all fabrication details, dimensions, and notes. Internal quality review ensures drawings are complete, coordinated, and free of conflicts before submission.

4

Delivery & Revisions

Completed takeoff reports and drawing packages are delivered in your preferred format — PDF, Excel, or CAD. We accommodate revision requests and resubmissions to keep your project moving forward.
