What We Offer

Custom Capabilities

01

Architectural Mesh Systems

We collaborate with architects and designers to create custom wire mesh facades, sun screens, and interior partitions. Our team can produce unique weave patterns, custom aperture shapes, and specialty finishes that transform functional mesh into striking architectural features for buildings of all types.

02

Specialized Security Fencing

For high-security applications — including airports, data centers, military installations, and critical infrastructure — we design and manufacture fencing systems with anti-climb features, integrated cable routing for intrusion detection, and reinforced post systems rated to withstand specified impact and wind loads.

03

Industrial Screening & Separation

We produce custom mesh screens for mining, aggregate processing, wastewater treatment, and food production. Each screen is engineered with the correct wire diameter, mesh opening, and material grade to optimize throughput, wear resistance, and separation accuracy for your specific application.

04

Custom Gates & Access Control

From ornamental estate gates to heavy-duty industrial swing and sliding gates, we fabricate custom gate systems complete with integrated access control mounting points, automation hardware provisions, and matching fence panel designs for a cohesive site appearance.

Our Process

From Idea to Installation

1

Requirements Analysis

We begin by understanding your project scope, performance requirements, environmental conditions, and design preferences through a detailed consultation with our engineering team.

2

Design & Engineering

Our engineers develop detailed technical drawings, material specifications, and structural calculations. We provide 3D renderings and samples where needed so you can review and approve the design before production begins.

3

Manufacturing & Finishing

Approved designs move into production with dedicated fabrication runs. We apply the specified surface treatment — galvanizing, powder coating, or other finishes — and conduct thorough quality checks at every stage.

4

Delivery & Support

Finished products are carefully packaged and shipped to your site. We provide installation guides, technical support, and on-site assistance when required to ensure proper fitting and long-term performance.
