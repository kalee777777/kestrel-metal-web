Highway Safety Barrier Installation Project

Australia Transportation Infrastructure
Back to Case Studies

Project Overview

A state road authority in Australia engaged Kestrel Metal to supply and install 3D wire panel fencing and razor wire topping as part of a major safety upgrade along a 200km highway network. The project was designed to strengthen perimeter security, control access along the corridor, and protect road users and adjacent infrastructure from unauthorised entry.

The scope of work spanned multiple sections of the highway, requiring a high-strength fencing solution that could be deployed reliably across varied terrain, from open rural stretches to elevated structures and restricted access zones. Every barrier system was engineered to meet AASHTO crash test standards, ensuring the fencing could withstand vehicle impacts and perform as a genuine safety barrier.

Kestrel Metal supplied 3D wire panel fencing manufactured with a hot-dip galvanised and PVC-coated finish, providing exceptional corrosion resistance and a long maintenance-free service life suitable for the harsh roadside environment. The addition of razor wire topping further enhanced the security of critical and high-risk locations along the route.

The Challenge

Highway safety barriers must satisfy stringent performance criteria, combining high-impact resistance with reliable containment to protect motorists and prevent cross-traffic intrusion. The selected system needed to meet AASHTO crash test standards, a requirement that ruled out many conventional fencing products not designed for vehicle impact loading.

The 200km corridor presented significant installation challenges, including variable soil conditions, steep embankments, and the need to maintain traffic flow throughout the construction works. The fencing had to be installed in long, continuous runs with consistent tension and alignment, demanding accurate fabrication and a modular system that could adapt to site conditions.

Durability was equally critical, with the barrier expected to perform over its full design life in a highway environment exposed to weathering, de-icing salts, airborne debris, and repeated impact. The chosen materials required a robust corrosion protection system to prevent premature degradation and minimise the lifecycle cost of maintenance and replacement.

Our Solution

Kestrel Metal supplied high-strength 3D wire panel fencing manufactured from rigid welded mesh panels with a three-dimensional stiffening profile, providing enhanced structural rigidity and anti-climb performance. The panels were hot-dip galvanised and finished with a PVC coating, delivering a durable, weather-resistant surface engineered for the demanding roadside environment.

Razor wire topping was installed along critical and high-security sections to provide an additional layer of perimeter protection, deterring unauthorised access and climbing attempts while complementing the physical strength of the panel system. The combined solution met AASHTO crash test standards, verified to safely contain and redirect impacting vehicles.

The modular panel design allowed for rapid, site-adaptive installation across the full 200km corridor, with consistent tension and alignment maintained throughout. The hot-dip galvanised and PVC-coated finish provides long-term corrosion resistance, reducing maintenance requirements and delivering a cost-effective, high-performance safety barrier for the highway upgrade.
