Fence Products
What types of fence panels do you offer?
We offer 3D wire panel fences, chain link fences, security fences (358 mesh, V-mesh, palisade), farm fences, temporary fences, and custom solutions. All panels are available in galvanized or PVC coated finishes.
What is the standard panel size for 3D wire fence?
Standard 3D wire panel sizes include 2500 x 2500mm, 2500 x 2300mm, 2500 x 2000mm, and 2500 x 1700mm. Custom sizes are available upon request with minimum order quantities.
How long does fence installation take?
Installation time varies by project size. A typical residential fence (50-100 meters) takes 2-3 days. Commercial projects may require 1-4 weeks depending on terrain and complexity.
What is the warranty on your fence products?
Our galvanized steel fences come with a 10-year warranty against corrosion. PVC coated products have a 15-year warranty for coating adhesion and UV resistance.
Do you provide fence installation services?
We provide detailed installation guides and technical support. For large projects, we can recommend certified installation partners in your region.
Wire Mesh Products
What is the difference between woven and welded wire mesh?
Woven wire mesh is made by interlocking wire strands (hexagonal, gabion), offering flexibility. Welded wire mesh has wires welded at intersections, providing rigidity and precise openings for construction applications.
What mesh sizes are available?
We offer mesh sizes from 1/4 inch (6mm) to 4 inch (100mm) openings. Wire diameters range from 0.5mm to 6.0mm depending on the application.
Can wire mesh be customized?
Yes, we offer full customization including mesh size, wire diameter, panel dimensions, material (carbon steel, stainless steel, galvanized), and surface treatment (HDG, PVC, powder coating).
What is the minimum order quantity?
Standard products have a minimum order of 100 panels or 50 rolls. Custom products require a minimum order of 500 panels. Sample orders are available for quality evaluation.
Wire Products
What is the difference between barbed wire and razor wire?
Barbed wire has twisted wire strands with barbs spaced at regular intervals, suitable for agricultural and general fencing. Razor wire features sharp blade-like edges formed from steel strips, providing superior security for high-threat applications.
What finishes are available for barbed wire?
We offer hot-dip galvanized (HDG), electro-galvanized, and PVC coated finishes. HDG provides 20+ years of corrosion resistance in normal environments.
Is razor wire suitable for residential use?
Razor wire is primarily designed for high-security applications (military, prisons, airports). For residential security, we recommend barbed wire or security fence panels as more appropriate solutions.
