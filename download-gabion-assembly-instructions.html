Instructions for assembling wire mesh gabion baskets including folding, lacing, stone filling, and lid securing.

Introduction

This guide provides step-by-step installation instructions for Kestrel Metal gabion box.

Tools Required

  • Measuring tape and string line
  • Spirit level (1.2m recommended)
  • Post hole digger or auger
  • Concrete mixer (for post foundations)
  • Safety equipment: gloves, safety glasses, steel-toe boots
  • Welding equipment (if required)

Before You Begin

Ensure the installation area is clear of obstacles and underground utilities have been identified. Check all materials against the delivery note and inspect for any damage before installation.

Installation Steps

Step 1: Site Preparation

Mark the fence or structure line using string lines and pegs. Clear vegetation and debris along the line. Ensure the ground is level; excavate high spots as needed.

Step 2: Foundation / Post Installation

Dig post holes to the required depth (typically 600-800mm for standard fencing). Space posts at the specified intervals. Place posts in holes, ensure vertical alignment with a spirit level, and pour concrete mix around posts. Allow concrete to cure for minimum 48 hours before proceeding.

Step 3: Assembly

Follow the specific assembly instructions for your product type. Ensure all connections are secure and components are properly aligned before tightening fixings.

Step 4: Final Inspection

Check all joints, fixings, and alignments. Verify that gates open and close freely if applicable. Make any necessary adjustments before sign-off.

For detailed diagrams and specifications, please refer to the full PDF document. If you encounter any issues during installation, contact our technical support team for assistance.
