Certificate Overview

This document contains official certification and compliance documentation for Kestrel Metal products and quality management systems.

Scope

The certifications covered in this document apply to the design, manufacture, and supply of wire mesh products, fencing systems, gabion products, and related metal products manufactured by Kestrel Metal Industrial Co., Ltd.

Key Compliance Standards

  • EN 10223-1: Steel wire and wire products for fencing
  • EN 10223-6: Steel wire and wire products — Chain link fencing
  • EN 10223-7: Steel wire and wire products — Welded mesh fencing
  • EN 10223-8: Steel wire and wire products — Welded mesh gabions
  • ASTM A370: Standard Test Methods and Definitions for Mechanical Testing
  • ASTM A123: Standard Specification for Zinc (Hot-Dip Galvanized) Coatings

Quality Objectives

  • Product conformity rate: ≥ 99.5%
  • On-time delivery: ≥ 97%
  • Customer satisfaction score: ≥ 4.5/5.0
  • Supplier non-conformance: ≤ 2%
