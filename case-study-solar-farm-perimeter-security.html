Chain Link Fencing Secures 500MW Solar Farm Perimeter

Australia Renewable Energy
Back to Case Studies

Project Overview

A 500MW solar farm in Australia required a comprehensive perimeter security solution to protect its extensive generation infrastructure across 800 acres. With panels, inverters, and high-voltage equipment spread over a vast site, the developer needed a secure and durable boundary that could discourage unauthorised access while keeping the facility safe from intruders and wildlife.

Kestrel Metal was engaged to design and supply a full perimeter fencing system combining PVC-coated chain link fencing, anti-climb barriers, and vehicle access gates. The solution needed to deliver a clear security line around the entire solar array while allowing authorised maintenance and emergency access at controlled points.

The remote location of the solar farm presented unique challenges, including extreme UV exposure, high winds, and the presence of native wildlife that could interfere with sensitive electrical equipment. Every component had to be selected for long-term durability with minimal maintenance under these demanding environmental conditions.

The Challenge

Solar farms span vast open areas that are inherently difficult to secure. The 800-acre site required a fencing line that could be installed efficiently across uneven terrain while maintaining a consistent and reliable barrier against both human intrusion and wildlife entry.

Kangaroos, wallabies, and other native animals can cause significant damage to solar infrastructure by moving between panel rows, and unauthorised visitors present both safety and security concerns around high-voltage equipment. The fencing needed to exclude wildlife effectively while providing anti-climb protection strong enough to deter determined intruders.

The harsh Australian climate added another layer of difficulty. Intense UV radiation can rapidly degrade standard fencing materials, and the facility's remote position meant that any maintenance would be costly and time-consuming. The developer required a solution with an extended service life that could withstand decades of weather exposure without compromising security.

Our Solution

Kestrel Metal supplied a PVC-coated chain link fencing system designed specifically for the challenges of large-scale renewable energy sites. The galvanised steel core combined with a PVC coating provides exceptional corrosion resistance and UV stability, ensuring the fence retains its strength and appearance across the full length of the site.

Anti-climb barriers were integrated along the top of the fencing line to prevent intruders from scaling the structure, while securely engineered vehicle access gates were positioned at designated entry points to control the movement of maintenance crews and emergency services. The gates were built to match the security rating of the surrounding fence.

For wildlife exclusion, the fencing was detailed to create a continuous barrier with no gaps that could allow animals to pass beneath or slip through. The combination of PVC-coated chain link, anti-climb protection, and controlled access gates delivers a complete, durable perimeter solution that keeps the solar farm secure and its generation infrastructure protected.
