Understanding KESTREL WELDED MESH 711-714: A Complete Product Guide

November 10, 2025 6 min read
Back to Blog & News

The Galvaplax Welded Mesh Series

The 711 series represents the foundation of Kestrel Metal's welded mesh product line, featuring PVC-coated electrically welded wire mesh panels with uniform square mesh openings. Engineered for versatility and long-term performance, these panels are manufactured using precision-controlled electrical resistance welding that ensures consistent weld strength at every intersection point. The Galvaplax 711 series is ideally suited for agricultural fencing, garden enclosures, and light industrial applications where reliable perimeter containment is required without compromising on visual appeal. Each panel is constructed from hot-dip galvanized core wire that provides exceptional corrosion resistance, then finished with a premium PVC coating available in green and black color options. This dual-layer protection system ensures a projected service life exceeding 20 years in standard environmental conditions, making the 711 series a cost-effective choice for projects that demand both durability and aesthetic integration with natural surroundings.

CombipIax 712 — Differentiated Mesh Patterns

The 712 series introduces an innovative approach to welded mesh design by featuring alternating mesh sizes within a single panel. This differentiated pattern provides enhanced visibility and airflow at the upper portion of the panel while maintaining smaller, tighter mesh at the bottom for robust ground-level security and containment. The CombipIax 712 design is particularly effective for animal enclosures where species-specific containment requirements must be balanced with the need for adequate ventilation and visual monitoring. Orchard protection installations benefit from the larger upper mesh openings, which allow pollinators and beneficial insects to pass freely while preventing birds and larger pests from accessing fruit crops. Perimeter fencing applications where both visibility and containment are equally important — such as residential estate boundaries, park enclosures, and commercial property perimeters — are ideally served by the 712 series. The graduated mesh pattern is achieved through advanced automated welding systems that maintain precise dimensional control across the transition zone, ensuring structural integrity throughout the panel.

Overplax 713 and Stilplax 714 — Enhanced Security Solutions

For applications requiring elevated security performance, the 713 and 714 series deliver purpose-engineered anti-climb and anti-intrusion capabilities. The Overplax 713 series features triple-folded selvedge edges that eliminate sharp wire ends while dramatically increasing panel rigidity along the vertical boundaries. The top edge of each 713 panel incorporates pointed vertical wire extensions that serve as an effective anti-climb deterrent, making unauthorized scaling extremely difficult without specialized equipment. The Stilplax 714 series builds upon the 713 platform by adding fish-hook profiles along the top edge, providing maximum anti-intrusion deterrence for the most demanding security environments. Both series are designed for high-security applications including industrial facilities, military installations, government properties, critical infrastructure protection zones, and correctional institutions. The enhanced edge treatments also improve the structural load-bearing capacity of each panel, allowing for taller fence installations without the need for intermediate horizontal support rails.

Technical Specifications and Selection Guide

KESTREL WELDED MESH panels in the 711-714 series are available in standard panel dimensions of 2500mm width by 2000mm height, with custom dimensions available upon request for projects with specific installation requirements. Wire diameters range from 2.0mm for lightweight residential applications to 5.0mm for high-security industrial installations, with intermediate sizes of 2.5mm, 3.0mm, 3.5mm, and 4.0mm available to match precise performance specifications. Mesh sizes span from 25mm x 25mm for fine containment applications to 200mm x 50mm for visibility-oriented installations, with the most popular configurations being 50mm x 50mm and 75mm x 75mm square openings. The PVC coating thickness is maintained at 0.4-0.6mm over the galvanized core wire, providing a total wire diameter that ensures both the required mechanical strength and long-term weather resistance.

Choosing the right welded mesh specification requires careful consideration of four key factors: the level of security required, the environmental conditions at the installation site, the expected service life of the fencing system, and any aesthetic requirements that must be met. Consulting with a qualified fencing specialist early in the project planning stage can prevent costly specification errors and ensure optimal long-term performance.

Quality Assurance and Compliance

Every KESTREL WELDED MESH panel in the 711-714 series is manufactured under ISO 9001 certified quality management systems, ensuring consistent product quality across every production batch. Our manufacturing facilities comply with European standards for welded wire mesh products, including EN 10223 for fence systems and EN 10244 for wire coating specifications. Each production batch undergoes comprehensive salt spray testing to verify a minimum of 1000 hours of corrosion resistance, exceeding the requirements of most international standards. Tensile strength verification is performed on every production batch using calibrated universal testing machines, with test results documented and retained for full traceability. Additionally, weld shear strength testing is conducted at regular intervals throughout each production run to ensure that the electrical resistance welding process maintains consistent bond integrity across all wire intersection points. This rigorous quality assurance program ensures that every panel delivered to our customers meets or exceeds the specified performance requirements.
