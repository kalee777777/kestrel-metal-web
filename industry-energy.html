Industry Challenges

Solving Energy's Toughest Problems

We understand the unique challenges utility operators and EPC contractors face — and we engineer solutions to address each one.

Electrical Safety Clearance

Substation and transmission fencing must maintain safe clearance zones from energized equipment to protect personnel and the public from arc flash and electrical hazards.

Custom-height fencing systems engineered to maintain NESC and IEEE minimum clearance distances, with non-conductive coating options for additional electrical safety.

Anti-Climb Security

Electrical substations and energy infrastructure are critical assets. Unauthorized access risks equipment damage, power outages, and serious injury or fatality.

358 anti-climb mesh fencing with narrow apertures prevents climbing and tool insertion, integrated with CCTV and SCADA security systems for layered substation protection.

Grounding & Lightning

Substation fencing must be integrated into the facility's grounding grid to manage fault currents and lightning strikes safely, requiring specialized bonding and grounding hardware.

Grounding-compatible fencing systems with engineered bonding points and grounding hardware designed for integration with substation ground grids and lightning protection.

Large-Scale Deployment

Utility-scale solar and wind projects span hundreds of hectares and require massive quantities of fencing delivered on tight project schedules across remote locations.

Turnkey supply and logistics with high-volume production capacity and dedicated project coordination ensures on-time delivery for utility-scale energy projects worldwide.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What fencing height is required for electrical substations?
NESC and IEEE standards typically require substation fencing of at least 2.1 meters (7 feet) with a 3-meter clearance zone from energized equipment. Some utilities require 2.4-meter or 3-meter fencing. We manufacture to your specific utility's clearance requirements.
Can your fencing be grounded for electrical safety?
Yes. Our substation fencing systems include grounding-compatible designs with bonding points for connection to the station grounding grid. This ensures electrical safety and lightning protection compliance with NESC requirements.
Do you supply fencing for utility-scale solar farms?
Yes. We provide chain link and welded mesh fencing for solar farm perimeters of any size. Our systems include animal exclusion designs that prevent wildlife from damaging panels while allowing unobstructed sunlight exposure.
Can your security fencing integrate with SCADA systems?
Yes. Our anti-climb 358 security fencing is designed to integrate with SCADA, CCTV, and intrusion detection systems. The narrow mesh allows sensor mounting and camera visibility while preventing unauthorized access to critical energy infrastructure.
