Product Overview

Strong, Durable Gabion Retaining Structures

Welded gabion boxes are rigid, box-shaped wire mesh containers manufactured using electric resistance welding at each wire intersection. Unlike woven gabion baskets, the welded construction creates a dimensionally stable, rigid structure that maintains its shape without bulging or deformation.

These welded gabion boxes are ideal for retaining walls, erosion control, riverbank protection, bridge abutments, and landscaping projects where structural integrity is critical. They comply with ASTM A974 and BS EN 10223-8 standards and are available in galvanized, PVC coated, and stainless steel finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about welded gabion boxes.

What are the standard sizes for welded gabion boxes?
Standard welded gabion boxes are available in a range of sizes from 0.125 m³ to 3.0 m³. The most common sizes include 1.0 x 1.0 x 0.5m (0.5 m³), 1.0 x 1.0 x 1.0m (1.0 m³), and 2.0 x 1.0 x 1.0m (2.0 m³). Custom sizes can be manufactured to meet specific project requirements.
How are welded gabion boxes assembled on site?
Welded gabion boxes arrive pre-fabricated as folded flat panels. On site, the assembly process involves: 1) Unfolding the panels to form the box shape, 2) Connecting the side panels to the base using the provided locking clips, 3) Filling the box with the specified stone or rock material, 4) Installing the top cover panel and securing with clips, 5) Connecting adjacent boxes together to form a continuous structure.
What type of stone should be used to fill welded gabion boxes?
The recommended stone size should be larger than the mesh opening to prevent stones from falling through. For gabion boxes with 50mm x 50mm mesh, use stone ranging from 80-150mm. For 75mm x 75mm mesh, use 100-200mm stone. For 100mm x 100mm mesh, use 150-250mm stone. Use durable, angular crushed stone for the best structural performance.
How long do welded gabion boxes last?
The service life of welded gabion boxes depends on the surface treatment and environmental conditions. Galvanized boxes typically last 15-25 years in normal outdoor conditions. PVC coated boxes provide 25-40 years of service. Stainless steel boxes offer the longest lifespan, exceeding 50 years in most environments. Proper installation and using the correct stone fill also contribute to longevity.
What accessories are needed for gabion box installation?
We supply a complete range of gabion accessories including: locking clips for panel assembly, spiral binders for securing connections, tensioning tools for tightening, installation gloves, and cut-resistant wire cutters. These accessories ensure proper and efficient installation of your gabion box structure.
Can welded gabion boxes be used in coastal environments?
Yes, welded gabion boxes can be used in coastal environments, but the correct surface treatment must be selected. For coastal areas with saltwater exposure, we strongly recommend PVC coated or stainless steel (316 grade) gabion boxes. Standard galvanized boxes may corrode rapidly in salt-spray environments and are not recommended for coastal applications.
