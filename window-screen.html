Let Air In, Keep Insects Out

Window screen is a mesh material used to cover openings in windows, doors, and other ventilation areas. It allows air and light to pass through while keeping insects, debris, and other unwanted elements out. Available in aluminum alloy (ASH, AS series) and stainless steel (SUS304, SUS316) materials to meet various application requirements.

Whether for residential homes or demanding coastal and industrial environments, our standardized widths and lengths make installation simple while delivering dependable, long-lasting protection.

Material Options

ASH Series (Aluminum Alloy): Lightweight and economical option ideal for residential insect screening. Features higher mesh density for better insect protection while maintaining good visibility and airflow.

AS Series (Standard Aluminum): General-purpose aluminum alloy window screen suitable for standard residential and light commercial applications. Offers good balance of durability and cost-effectiveness.

SUS304 (Stainless Steel 304): Corrosion-resistant stainless steel for coastal areas, commercial buildings, and high-traffic installations. Provides superior durability and longer service life.

SUS316 (Stainless Steel 316): Marine-grade stainless steel with superior corrosion resistance for harsh environments, industrial applications, and saltwater exposure areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about window screen mesh.

What is the difference between ASH and AS aluminum window screen?
ASH (Aluminum Alloy) series uses a higher-density aluminum alloy weave, delivering better insect protection with finer mesh while maintaining good visibility and airflow. It is ideal for residential use. AS (Standard Aluminum) series is a general-purpose aluminum alloy window screen suitable for standard residential and light commercial applications, offering a good balance of durability and cost-effectiveness.
When should I choose stainless steel (SUS304/SUS316) window screen?
Choose SUS304 for coastal areas, commercial buildings and high-traffic installations where superior durability and corrosion resistance are required. Choose SUS316 (marine grade) for harsh environments, industrial applications and saltwater exposure areas, as its added molybdenum provides the best corrosion resistance against chloride pitting.
What roll sizes are available?
Window screen is available in standard widths from 0.61m to 2.64m and roll lengths from 30m to 500m. Common specifications include ASH2011, ASH2411, AS150, SUS304 and SUS316 across mesh sizes of 12x12 to 24x22. Custom widths and lengths are available upon request to match your window and door dimensions.
How do I measure and install window screen?
Measure the width and height of the window opening, then add 2-3cm on each side for overlap. Cut the screen to size using scissors or a utility knife, lay it over the frame and fasten with spline in a screen groove or with staples. Trim the excess. Standardized roll widths (0.61-2.64m) let you select the closest width and cut to the required length for a clean fit.
