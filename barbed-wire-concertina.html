Product Information

Concertina barbed wire is a type of barbed wire that is formed into large coils which can be rapidly deployed for security purposes. Unlike traditional straight-line barbed wire, concertina wire is clipped at intervals to maintain its coiled shape, creating a formidable physical barrier that is extremely difficult to breach or cut through.

Each coil is clipped with galvanized steel clips at regular intervals along the spiral, ensuring the coil maintains its cylindrical shape and structural integrity. When stretched, the concertina coil expands to form an effective barrier that can cover significant distances with minimal installation effort.
