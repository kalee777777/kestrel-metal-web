Vibrating Screen Replacement Boosts Copper Mining Efficiency

Australia Mining & Aggregate Processing
Back to Case Studies

Project Overview

Kestrel Metal was engaged by a regional copper mining operation in Australia to replace a degraded vibrating screen deck on its primary processing line. The original screen panels had suffered extensive wear and reduced apertures over years of continuous abrasive service, causing material to clog and limiting the throughput of the crushing and classification circuit.

Using custom-manufactured wedge wire screens and heavy-duty welded mesh panels, Kestrel Metal supplied a tailored deck replacement engineered to match the exact dimensions and loading conditions of the existing vibrating screen frame, ensuring a direct drop-in fit with no modifications to the surrounding structure.

The project's objective was twofold: restore screening capacity to design levels and deliver a far more durable deck that could withstand the abrasive copper ore without the frequent panel failures the mine had previously experienced. Every component was manufactured with a zinc-aluminium alloy and polymer coating to resist corrosion in the humid, abrasive mine environment.

The Challenge

The mining operation relied on a single primary vibrating screen to feed its downstream concentration circuit, so any downtime directly impacted production output and revenue. The previous screen panels were failing at an accelerated rate, with premature wear opening gaps that allowed oversized material to pass through and compromise the quality of the final product.

Abrasive copper ore particles, combined with constant vibration and repeated impact loading, demanded a screening surface with superior wear resistance and structural rigidity. Off-the-shelf panels had proven inadequate, wearing unevenly and clogging frequently, which forced the maintenance team into costly and unpredictable stop-start interventions.

Replacing the entire screen deck required precision manufacturing to maintain accurate aperture size and open area, essential for achieving efficient classification while minimising the blinding and pegging that had plagued the operation. The mine also required a solution that maximised service life to reduce the frequency and cost of future replacements.

Our Solution

Kestrel Metal custom-manufactured wedge wire screen panels designed with precisely controlled aperture widths to deliver accurate particle sizing and maximum open area. The wedge profile provides a self-cleaning action that significantly reduces material blinding, allowing fines and moisture to fall freely through the screen without clogging the apertures.

Heavy-duty welded mesh panels were supplied for the higher-impact zones, where their rigid structure and reinforced wire diameter provide the strength required to withstand repeated ore impact while maintaining dimensional stability under full load. Combined, the two panel types created a deck optimised for both accuracy and durability.

The installation of the new screen deck improved screening efficiency by 35% and dramatically extended the operating service life compared with the previous panels. The zinc-aluminium alloy and polymer coating protected the screening media against abrasive wear and corrosion, reducing maintenance frequency and delivering a significant reduction in the mine's operating cost per tonne of material processed.
