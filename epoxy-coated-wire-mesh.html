Epoxy Coating Technology

Epoxy coated wire mesh is produced by applying a thermosetting epoxy resin coating to the wire mesh through an electrostatic dipping or fluidized bed process. The epoxy coating forms a tough, protective layer that fully encapsulates the individual wires, providing a continuous barrier against moisture, chemicals, and corrosive elements. The coating is cured at controlled temperatures to ensure maximum adhesion and durability.

This advanced coating technology significantly extends the service life of wire mesh in demanding environments where standard galvanized or uncoated mesh would corrode rapidly.

Applications

Epoxy coated wire mesh is widely used in architectural facades and cladding, industrial equipment guards and safety barriers, marine components and offshore structures, and protective fencing for highways, railways and public utilities where long-term durability and appearance retention are essential.
