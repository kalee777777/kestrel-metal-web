Product Information

Reno mattresses, also known as gabion mattresses, are thin, flat gabion units manufactured from hexagonal double-twisted woven wire mesh. Unlike standard gabion baskets, reno mattresses have a significantly lower profile, typically ranging from 300mm to 500mm in height, making them ideal for applications requiring flexible, erosion-resistant coverage over large surface areas.

The primary function of reno mattresses is to provide scour protection and erosion control for hydraulic structures, riverbeds, and channel linings. Their low-profile design allows them to conform closely to the underlying terrain, providing continuous protection against water-induced erosion while maintaining natural groundwater flow through the permeable stone-filled structure.

Reno mattresses are delivered flat-packed for efficient transportation and can be easily assembled on-site. Once filled with natural stone, they create a stable, flexible lining that allows vegetation to establish over time, promoting ecological restoration and blending seamlessly with the natural environment.

Application

Reno mattresses are extensively used in channel lining, riverbank protection, scour protection, coastal defense, outlet structures, and dam toe protection. Their flexibility and durability make them the preferred solution for hydraulic engineers requiring reliable, long-term erosion control in rivers, canals, and coastal environments.
