CE / UKCA / REACH Compliance Checklist for Wire Mesh & Fencing (2026 Guide)

August 21, 2026 9 min read
Back to Blog & News

CE marking, UKCA marking, and REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals) regulation are the three mandatory compliance frameworks for welded wire mesh, chain link fence, gabion boxes, razor wire, and 3D wire panels exported from China to the European Union and United Kingdom. CE requires EN 10223 (wire mesh fence) or EN 10244 (galvanized wire coatings) conformance plus a valid Declaration of Performance (DoP); UKCA applies equivalent UK-designated standards; REACH restricts SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) in PVC coating resins, chromate passivation layers, and lead-based pigments to below 0.1% w/w. Kestrel Metal manufactures ISO 9001:2015 certified fencing products meeting all three frameworks, supplies DoP documents on request, ships FOB Tianjin/Shanghai with 15–25 day lead time.

What CE, UKCA, and REACH Actually Require

CE marking is the European Union's primary conformity assessment mark for goods placed on the EU single market, covering health, safety, and environmental protection requirements. For construction products such as wire mesh and fencing, CE marking falls under the Construction Products Regulation (CPR, EU 305/2011), which requires the manufacturer to assess the product against a harmonized European standard (EN), operate a documented Factory Production Control (FPC) system, and draw up a Declaration of Performance (DoP) that states the declared performance characteristics for each essential characteristic listed in that standard. The DoP must be supplied to the importer or distributor with every shipment and must reference the specific EN standard, the notified body involved (where system 1 or 2+ applies), and the batch or production year covered.

UKCA marking (UK Conformity Assessed) is the mandatory conformity mark for most goods placed on the Great Britain market (England, Wales and Scotland) following the UK's withdrawal from the EU. The technical requirements for UKCA are substantively equivalent to CE: the same EN standards (designated as "BS EN" UK-designated standards) apply, the Factory Production Control requirements mirror CPR Annex V, and the UK DoP follows a near-identical template to the EU DoP. Kestrel Metal issues UKCA-compatible DoP documents referencing UK-designated standards where applicable, enabling importers to clear goods into either the EU or GB market from a single manufacturing run when the same test evidence applies to both regimes.

REACH (EC 1907/2006) is the EU's chemicals regulation that governs the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemical substances imported into or manufactured within the EU. Unlike CE and UKCA, REACH applies to substances and mixtures, not to end products as a whole. For wire mesh and fencing products, the key REACH obligations concern the zinc passivation chemistries used on galvanized surfaces, the PVC plastisol or polyester powder coating resins applied over the steel substrate, and any organic pigments or heavy-metal driers compounded into those coatings. Specifically, any substance on the REACH Candidate List of SVHCs (Substances of Very High Concern) must be notified in the supply chain if present above 0.1% w/w in any article; certain Annex XIV substances also require authorization from ECHA before they can be placed on the EU market above stated thresholds.

REACH SVHC Testing: What to Worry About

The REACH Candidate List of SVHCs is updated twice a year by ECHA and typically runs to in excess of 250 substances as of 2026. For wire mesh and fencing products, the most critical SVHCs to screen for fall into three material categories: PVC coating stabilizers, chromate-based conversion coatings, and inorganic pigments used in colored fence systems. Lead compounds (used historically as heat stabilizers in PVC and as pigments in RAL 8004 brown and RAL 6005 green formulations) and cobalt(II) salts (sometimes present as drying promoters in liquid coating systems) are prominent on the SVHC list and are routinely flagged by EU customs during document reviews.

Kestrel Metal's compliance position is unambiguous and fully tested. All PVC coated wire mesh and chain link fence supplied by Kestrel Metal for the EU and UK markets uses only REACH-compliant PVC resins stabilized with calcium-zinc (CaZn) systems rather than lead or organotin stabilizers. The black, green (RAL 6005), and brown (RAL 8004) PVC finishes and any polyester powder coatings are pigmented exclusively with stable iron oxides and approved inorganic pigment grades that do not contain lead, cadmium, or Class 1A / 1B carcinogenic colorants. Chromate passivation is not applied to galvanized wire or mesh by default — Kestrel Metal uses chromate-free passivation chemistries for all hot-dip galvanized fence components intended for EU/UK destinations.

Third-party SVHC screening is performed quarterly by an accredited CNAS laboratory against the latest published Candidate List, covering representative coupons from each of our galvanized-only, PVC coated, and powder coated product lines. Test reports consistently confirm that all listed SVHC substances are present at concentrations well below the 0.1% w/w threshold per article, which means no SVHC communication obligation is triggered in the supply chain and no Annex XIV authorization is required for any substance intentionally used in our fence manufacturing process. Full SVHC declarations and laboratory test reports are available on request and are bundled with every commercial shipment dossier for EU and UK importers who need to maintain compliance records on file for ten years.

The Declaration of Performance (DoP) — What Your Importer Needs

The Declaration of Performance is the legal document required by the EU Construction Products Regulation (CPR) and by the equivalent UK CPR regime for any construction product covered by a harmonized standard. A DoP is not a test report in itself; rather, it is the manufacturer's legally binding statement of the product's declared performance values against each of the essential characteristics defined in the relevant EN standard. For fence and wire mesh products these characteristics typically include wire nominal diameter, zinc coating mass per unit area, weld shear strength, mesh opening tolerance, tensile strength, and — where applicable — PVC coating thickness and adhesion class.

Kestrel Metal provides a signed DoP with every commercial shipment destined for the EU or UK, covering each product line included in the order. Each DoP lists the specific EN or BS EN standard under which the product is declared (e.g., EN 10223-4 for welded wire mesh panels, EN 10223-6 for chain link fence, EN 10244-1 for galvanized wire coatings), references the specific batch numbers and production dates included in the shipment, and cross-references the corresponding Factory Production Control plan and internal audit record number. DoPs also include our ISO 9001:2015 certificate reference, the FPC system identification, and — where a system 2+ or system 1 assessment is performed — the identification number and name of the notified or approved body that carried out the surveillance audit.

Importers and distributors placing Kestrel Metal products on the EU or UK single market are expected to retain the DoP for at least ten years after the last unit of the corresponding batch is placed on the market, and to make a copy available to their national market surveillance authority on request. Kestrel Metal maintains a full archiving system for all DoPs, test certificates, and FPC audit records for the same ten-year retention period, so duplicate copies can be issued retrospectively if importer records are misplaced during warehouse moves or ERP migrations.

HS Codes & Customs Documentation Reminders

Correctly classified Harmonized System (HS) codes are a prerequisite for smooth customs clearance and accurate duty calculation at the EU or UK port of entry. The following HS codes are the ones most commonly used by Kestrel Metal and our EU/UK importer partners for fence and wire mesh shipments. Always confirm the final classification and duty rate with your customs broker or freight forwarder before the ship sails, as national tariff preferences and anti-dumping measures can vary by product and by EU member state for specific steel product categories.

730890 — Fence structures (including prefabricated 3D wire panel fence, palisade fencing, complete fence kits with posts and fittings) and parts of structures of iron or steel, not elsewhere specified. Used for shipments where the dominant item is an assembled or kit-form perimeter fence system rather than raw mesh in roll form.

731441 — Welded galvanized wire mesh (not further worked than welded or galvanized), with wire diameter up to and including 3 mm, mesh size not exceeding 100 cm². Used for plain welded wire mesh rolls and panels supplied without posts or clamps.

731442 — Welded wire mesh, coated with plastics (PVC coated / polyester powder coated welded mesh, including PVC coated chain link fence where the dominant classification criterion is the welded or woven mesh body). Confirm with your broker whether chain link diamond mesh should be classified under 731442 or under the woven mesh codes below.

731419 — Woven wire mesh (not welded) of iron or steel, including hexagonally woven gabion wire mesh, woven wire cloth, and chain link (cyclone) woven diamond mesh rolls classified as woven rather than welded fabric. Gabion boxes supplied as folded woven mesh baskets are typically classified under this subheading or under 730890 depending on importer practice; Kestrel Metal's commercial invoices always state both the HS code used and a detailed goods description to support broker classification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does Kestrel Metal supply CE-marked wire mesh and fencing products?

A: Yes, all wire mesh and fencing products supplied by Kestrel Metal for the EU and UK markets are manufactured to applicable EN standards (EN 10223 for mesh, EN 10244 for zinc coatings) and a Declaration of Performance (DoP) is provided with every shipment.

Q: What is the difference between CE and UKCA marking?

A: CE marking is recognized for goods placed on the European Union single market; UKCA marking is the mandatory conformity mark for most goods placed on the Great Britain market (England, Wales, Scotland). The technical requirements (EN standards used, test evidence) are largely equivalent, and Kestrel Metal can issue DoP documents referencing both regimes for split shipments.

Q: Are your PVC coated products REACH compliant?

A: Yes. Kestrel Metal's PVC coated wire mesh and chain link fence use only REACH-compliant resins without lead-based stabilizers or Class 1A/1B carcinogenic pigments. All SVHC (Annex XIV) substances are below the 0.1% w/w threshold. A REACH declaration letter is available on request.

Q: What documentation do you provide for compliance records?
