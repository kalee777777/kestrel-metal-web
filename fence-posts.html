Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about fence post selection, sizing, and installation.

What is the difference between round, square, and rectangular posts?
Round posts (Ø48mm / Ø60mm) are the most economical choice for general fencing. Square posts (50×50 / 60×60mm) offer a clean modern appearance with excellent stability. Rectangular posts (40×60 up to 80×100mm) provide enhanced rigidity for high-security and heavy-duty applications. Choose your profile based on the load demands and aesthetic of your project.
What steel grades are used for your fence posts?
Our fence posts are manufactured from Q195, Q235, and Q345 low carbon steel. Q235 is the standard general-purpose grade for most fencing. Q345 offers higher strength for heavy-duty and high-security applications. Q195 is used where greater ductility and forming ease are needed. All posts undergo precision welding and rigorous quality inspection.
What finishes are available and which is best?
Three finishes are offered: hot-dip galvanized for maximum corrosion resistance in exposed and coastal environments; PVC coated for a smooth, low-maintenance colored finish (green, black, and more); and powder coated for a UV-stable, scratch-resistant appearance. For aggressive environments choose HDG or PVC; for aesthetics choose powder coating.
What post lengths and wall thicknesses can you supply?
Standard lengths range from 1.5m to 3.0m with wall thicknesses from 1.2mm to 3.0mm. Custom lengths, thicknesses, and drilling options are available upon request. For heavy fencing, choose thicker walls and rectangular sections to handle greater tension and impact without bending.
How do I choose the right post for my fence?
Consider fence height, wind load, and mesh tension. Taller fences and those holding heavy or high-tension mesh benefit from square or rectangular posts with thicker walls. Spacing posts closer together increases strength. For cyclonic or coastal areas, opt for hot-dip galvanized or PVC coating to resist corrosion.
Can posts be supplied with pre-drilled holes or fittings?
Yes. Posts can be manufactured with pre-drilled holes for bolts, clips, and fittings, or pre-fitted with caps, bases, and brackets depending on your fence design. Providing installation drawings or a bill of materials lets us prepare ready-to-erect posts for faster, cleaner installation.
How long do galvanized or coated posts last?
Hot-dip galvanized posts typically last 25 years or more in most environments. PVC and powder coated posts offer comparable longevity where the coating remains intact, with the added benefit of color retention. The zinc layer provides sacrificial corrosion protection; avoid scratching coated surfaces during installation to maximize life.
Are fence posts suitable for both residential and industrial projects?
Absolutely. Our fence posts are used across residential yards, commercial and industrial sites, highways, farms, and parks. By selecting the appropriate profile, thickness, and finish, the same post family supports everything from decorative garden fencing to heavy-duty security fences.
