Double Twist Barbed Wire

Double twist barbed wire consists of two strands of wire twisted together with barbs wrapped around them at regular intervals. The double-strand construction delivers significantly greater strength and durability than single twist barbed wire — if one strand is cut, the wire stays intact, making it the most widely used barbed wire type for farms and security fencing.

Product Information

Double twist barbed wire consists of two strands of wire twisted together with barbs wired around them at regular intervals. The double-strand construction provides significantly greater strength and durability compared to single twist barbed wire, making it the most widely used type of barbed wire worldwide.

The double twist design ensures that if one strand is cut, the wire remains intact due to the interlocking twist, providing enhanced security and longer service life.

Benefits

  • ✓Double-strand construction provides superior tensile strength and durability.
  • ✓If one strand is cut, the wire remains intact due to the interlocking twist.
  • ✓Available in a wide range of wire gauges from 12 BWG to 15-1/2 BWG.
  • ✓Barb spacing options of 3", 4", 5" and 6" for different security requirements.
  • ✓Galvanized coating provides excellent corrosion and weather resistance.

Application

Double twist barbed wire is widely used in agriculture fencing, livestock containment, grassland boundary, military defense, private property, highway and railway fencing, and other applications requiring reliable perimeter protection.
