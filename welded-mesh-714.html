Product Information

The KESTREL WELDED MESH 714 (Stilplax series) features rigid welded mesh panels specifically engineered for security fencing and perimeter protection. The defining characteristic is the fish-hook profile along the top edge, where each vertical wire is bent into a hook shape that effectively deters climbing and intrusion attempts.

The rigid panel construction eliminates the need for intermediate horizontal rails, reducing installation time and material costs while maintaining high load-bearing capacity. This series is the preferred choice for high-security installations where tamper resistance is critical.
