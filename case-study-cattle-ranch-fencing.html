Hinge Joint Field Fence Secures 10,000-Acre Cattle Ranch

Australia Agriculture & Livestock
Back to Case Studies

Project Overview

A 10,000-acre cattle ranch in Australia required a complete fencing solution to manage its large livestock herd and protect the property boundary. With vast open pastures and demanding seasonal weather conditions, the ranch needed a fence that was both strong enough to contain cattle and durable enough to last for decades with minimal upkeep.

Kestrel Metal was engaged to design and supply the full fencing package, combining hinge joint field fence for livestock containment, barbed wire for property boundary protection, and custom-built gates tailored to the ranch's operational needs. The solution was developed to deliver reliable performance across the entire property.

The scale of the project meant that every component had to balance strength, cost-effectiveness, and ease of installation. The ranch management required a system that a local fencing contractor could install efficiently while guaranteeing a consistent standard of livestock security across the full 10,000 acres.

The Challenge

Australian cattle properties endure some of the harshest environmental conditions of any agricultural operation. Extreme heat, intense UV exposure, heavy winds, and occasional flooding can rapidly degrade standard fencing materials, forcing cattle producers into costly and frequent replacement cycles.

Containing cattle across nearly 16,000 hectares requires a fence that can withstand animals pressing against it during mustering and grazing, while also preventing escapes that could threaten both the livestock and neighbouring landholders. The boundary line also needed barbed wire protection to discourage wildlife and trespassers.

Access across the property needed to be carefully managed with custom gates. The ranch required gates that could handle regular use by heavy vehicles and trailers, open and close reliably in extreme weather, and be positioned to support efficient cattle mustering and movement between paddocks.

Our Solution

Kestrel Metal supplied a complete fencing system built from hot-dip galvanised steel for superior corrosion resistance and long service life. Hinge joint field fence was installed along paddock lines, offering the flexibility of hinge joints that allow the fence to flex under pressure from cattle and thermal movement without permanently distorting or failing.

Barbed wire was installed along the property boundary to provide an additional deterrent layer against wildlife and prevent unauthorised intrusion, while the custom gates were fabricated to match the strength rating of the surrounding fence and engineered for smooth operation under heavy use in all weather conditions.

The combination of hinge joint field fence, barbed wire, and durable custom gates delivers a complete, long-lasting fencing solution that keeps the cattle securely contained, defines the property boundary clearly, and supports efficient day-to-day ranch operations on the 10,000-acre property.
