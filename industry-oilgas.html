Industry Overview

Securing Critical Energy Infrastructure

Oil and gas facilities operate some of the most sensitive and hazardous industrial environments in the world. Refineries, petrochemical plants, and chemical processing sites demand perimeter security and operational mesh products that withstand corrosive chemicals, salt spray, and H2S-laden atmospheres while meeting stringent API, OSHA, and IEC safety standards. Kestrel Metal engineers fencing and enclosure systems purpose-built for protecting critical energy infrastructure.

From high-security anti-climb perimeter fencing around refinery boundaries to galvanized grating and mesh platforms for elevated walkways, our products are manufactured to deliver long service life in the harshest conditions. 316L stainless steel options, heavy hot-dip galvanizing, and fire-rated mesh systems ensure compliance with hazardous area classifications and blast-resistant design requirements across downstream and midstream operations.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials do you recommend for H2S and sour gas environments?
For sour service environments, we recommend 316L stainless steel or specialty alloys meeting NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 requirements. These materials resist sulfide stress cracking and provide long-term performance in H2S-rich atmospheres.
Can your security fencing integrate with CCTV and intrusion detection systems?
Yes. Our 358 anti-climb security fencing is designed to integrate with CCTV cameras, infrared beams, and intrusion detection systems. The narrow mesh apertures prevent climbing while allowing clear camera visibility through the fence line.
Do you provide fire-rated mesh systems for hazardous areas?
Yes. We supply fire-rated and blast-resistant mesh systems for Zone 1 and Zone 2 hazardous areas. These systems meet API and IEC standards for use in petrochemical processing and refining facilities.
What documentation do you provide for compliance audits?
We provide full compliance documentation including material certificates, coating specifications, dimensional reports, and certificates of conformance to API, NACE, and ISO standards. All documentation is available for your regulatory and safety audits.
