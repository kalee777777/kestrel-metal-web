FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What wire diameter do I need for concrete reinforcement?
Wire diameter depends on the structural engineering specification. Common reinforcement mesh uses 4-12mm wire. Consult your structural engineer or our technical team for project-specific recommendations meeting ASTM A1064 or BS 4483.
Can temporary fencing be reused across multiple project sites?
Yes. Our 3D panel temporary fencing is designed for portability and reuse. The panels are lightweight, stackable, and feature durable powder-coated finishes that withstand repeated handling and relocation between sites.
Do you provide mill test certificates?
Yes, every batch of reinforcement mesh and fencing products ships with full material traceability including mill test certificates, chemical composition reports, and mechanical property test results.
What is the typical lead time for bulk construction orders?
Standard catalog products ship within 7-15 days. Large custom orders typically require 20-35 days depending on quantity and specifications. Contact us with your project schedule for a precise delivery estimate.
