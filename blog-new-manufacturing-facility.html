Kestrel Metal Expands Production Capacity with New Manufacturing Facility

April 26, 2025 4 min read
Back to Blog & News

A New Chapter in Manufacturing Excellence

Kestrel Metal is proud to announce the opening of our newest manufacturing facility, a state-of-the-art 50,000 square meter production complex strategically located in an established industrial zone with excellent logistics access to major highways, rail networks, and deep-water port facilities. This $25 million investment represents the single largest expansion in our company's history and effectively doubles our total production capacity for wire mesh and fencing products. The new facility has been designed from the ground up to accommodate growing global demand while maintaining the precision and quality standards that our customers have come to expect from Kestrel Metal over the past three decades.

State-of-the-Art Equipment and Technology

The new facility is equipped with the latest advancements in wire mesh manufacturing technology, sourced from leading European equipment manufacturers. Our new automated welding lines feature computer-controlled precision welding systems that deliver consistent, high-quality welds at speeds significantly faster than conventional equipment. Advanced PVC extrusion coating systems apply uniform polymer coatings with precise thickness control, ensuring superior corrosion protection and aesthetic finish across every product. Computer-controlled hot-dip galvanizing baths maintain exact zinc bath temperatures and immersion times, guaranteeing compliance with international galvanizing standards. CNC cutting and bending equipment rounds out our capabilities, enabling us to produce custom configurations with manufacturing tolerances held within plus or minus 0.5mm.

Expanding Product Capabilities

One of the most significant advantages of the new facility is the expanded range of product dimensions and specifications we can now offer. The enlarged production floor and upgraded equipment enable us to manufacture wire mesh panels in sizes up to 3 meters by 4 meters, a substantial increase from our previous maximum dimensions. We can now work with heavier wire diameters up to 8mm, opening up new possibilities for heavy-duty industrial and infrastructure applications. The facility also enables the launch of entirely new product lines, including architectural wire mesh for facade and interior design applications, as well as specialized filter elements for industrial processing. With streamlined production workflows and optimized material handling systems, standard products now ship within 7 business days, significantly faster than our previous lead times.

Sustainability at the Core

Environmental responsibility has been a central design principle throughout the planning and construction of our new manufacturing facility. A rooftop solar panel array generates approximately 30% of the facility's total energy requirements, reducing our dependence on grid electricity and lowering our carbon footprint. A closed-loop water recycling system captures, treats, and reuses process water throughout the facility, dramatically reducing freshwater consumption and eliminating wastewater discharge. Our galvanizing line incorporates an advanced zinc capture and recycling system that recovers excess zinc from the dipping process, achieving a zinc utilization rate exceeding 98%. These sustainability initiatives are part of our broader commitment to achieve carbon-neutral manufacturing operations by 2028, a target we are actively on track to meet.

What This Means for Our Customers

The expansion of our manufacturing capabilities translates directly into tangible benefits for our customers worldwide. Shorter lead times mean projects can proceed on schedule without delays caused by material availability. Our expanded product range provides architects, engineers, and contractors with a wider selection of specifications to choose from, reducing the need for costly custom modifications. Economies of scale achieved through higher production volumes enable us to offer more competitive pricing without compromising on quality. A dedicated quality control laboratory within the new facility houses advanced testing equipment including salt spray chambers for accelerated corrosion testing and universal tensile testing machines for verifying mechanical properties. We warmly invite all of our customers and partners to visit the new facility and see our expanded capabilities firsthand.
