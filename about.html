Who We Are

About Kestrel Metal

Located in Anping, Hebei Province — the renowned "Wire Mesh Capital of the World" — Kestrel Metal is a professional manufacturer dedicated to producing premium wire mesh and fencing products.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, high-quality wire mesh and fencing products that meet international standards, enabling our customers to build safer, more secure, and more efficient structures worldwide.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in the wire mesh industry, recognized for innovation, quality excellence, and sustainable manufacturing practices that create long-term value for our partners.

Our Journey

Company Milestones

2003

Company Founded

Kestrel Metal was established in Anping, Hebei, China, starting as a small wire mesh workshop.

2008

Export to Europe

Began exporting products to European markets, marking the start of our international journey.

2013

New Production Facility

Relocated to a modern 20,000 sqm production facility with automated manufacturing lines.

2018

ISO & CE Certification

Achieved ISO 9001 and CE certifications, validating our quality management and product standards.

2023

30+ Countries Served

Expanded global reach to serve customers in over 30 countries across 6 continents.
